## Planned topics

- Create or edit a member discount as a fixed amount or percentage.
- Set optional start and end dates for when the discount can be used.
- Apply an active discount from the invoice builder. Discounts are not applied automatically.
- Review the discount on the invoice and receipt.
- Understand how to remove a discount from the member's profile.
