Create a profile when you add a student, contact, or prospective student to Kanri. The first screen asks which kind of profile you want to create.

## Before you start

You need the **Manage Members** permission to add members. If you don't see a **New Member** button on the Members page, ask your school administrator to check your [role permissions](/settings/roles/).

## Create a profile

1. Go to **Members** in the main navigation.
2. Click **New Member** in the top right.
3. Choose **Member**, **Lead** (if available on your plan), or **Contact**.
4. Enter the person's details (see the field reference below).
5. Click **Create Member**.

Kanri then guides you through any relevant setup steps. A contact goes straight to their profile. A member can continue by adding contacts, completing an evaluation if your school uses one, and setting up memberships or billing. A lead goes through a shorter setup.

## Field reference

Only **First Name** and **Last Name** are required. Everything else is optional and can be filled in later.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **First Name** | Required. |
| **Last Name** | Required. |
| **Family Status** | The member's family situation: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Separated, Common Law, or Other. |
| **Gender** | Male, Female, or Other. |
| **Birthday** | Used in the [Birthday report](/reports/other/birthdays/) and shown as an age on the profile. |
| **Phone** | Used for SMS messages. Must be unique within your school. Kanri shows an error if another profile already has this number. |
| **Email** | Used for sending emails and for [portal login](/members/invite-member-portal/). Must be unique within your school. |
| **Lead details** | When you choose **Lead**, you can also record their status, programs of interest, and how they heard about your school. Lead-management features require a Professional or Enterprise plan. |

:::tip
If you're in a hurry, enter just the first and last name. You can add other details later by [editing the profile](/members/edit-member/).
:::

## Member types

Choose the profile type when you create it. Kanri also updates a profile's type when its role changes:

- New student profiles are **Members**. A membership or subscription can also change a Lead, Contact, or Staff profile to a Member.
- Choose **Lead** for a prospective student. Lead-management features require a Professional or Enterprise plan.
- Choose **Contact** for a parent, guardian, or another person connected to a member.
- A Lead can become **Staff** when you assign them a staff role. Staff access is managed separately from member profiles.

See the [Members overview](/members/) for more about profile types.

## What happens after you save

When Kanri creates the profile:

- A unique **member number** is automatically assigned. Members can use it, or search by first name, to sign in at the [kiosk](/kiosk/).
- If the email or phone number matches any existing unassigned message conversations, Kanri links them to the new profile.

For a new student, these are common next steps:

1. [Assign a membership](/members/memberships/assign-membership/) to enrol them in a program.
2. [Create a subscription](/members/subscriptions/create-subscription/) to set up recurring billing.
3. [Add a payment method](/members/subscriptions/add-payment-method/) if you want Kanri to collect payments automatically.
4. [Upload a photo](/members/edit-member/) for kiosk identification.
5. [Invite them to the portal](/members/invite-member-portal/) so they can log in.

:::note
Adding the member's address is done separately from the main profile form. Once the profile is saved, open it and click the **Address** card to add address details.
:::
