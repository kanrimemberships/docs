Delete an attendance entry when it was added by mistake.

## Before you delete attendance

Deleting attendance is a **soft delete**. That means the entry is removed from the normal list, but Kanri keeps a record in the background.

Deleting attendance can affect:

- attendance totals
- level progress
- reports that use attendance data
- sessions remaining on a block-style membership

## Delete an attendance entry

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Attendance**.
3. Find the entry you want to remove.
4. Click **Delete** on that entry.
5. Review the confirmation screen.
6. Click **Delete** again to confirm.

:::caution
Only delete attendance if you are sure the entry should not be there. If the member did attend, it is better to leave the record in place.
:::

## Check the member after deleting

After you delete attendance:

1. Review the member's attendance list.
2. Check their [membership](/members/memberships/) if you track level progress or sessions remaining.
3. Re-add the attendance correctly if needed.
