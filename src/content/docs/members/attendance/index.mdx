Attendance helps you track how often a member trains in each [membership](/members/memberships/).

In Kanri, attendance is recorded **per membership**, not just per member. This matters when one person is enrolled in more than one [program](/programs/). For example, a member could have one attendance history for Adult Karate and another for Weapons.

## Understand how attendance works

- Each attendance entry belongs to one membership.
- A member can have attendance across multiple memberships.
- You can only add attendance to an **active** membership.
- When you add attendance manually, the class must belong to the same program as the selected membership.
- Attendance can count toward level progress, unless the class has **counts towards attendance** turned off.
- If the membership tracks sessions, Kanri reduces **Sessions Remaining** when attendance is added.

:::note
Attendance and [subscriptions](/members/subscriptions/) are separate. Attendance follows the member's enrolment in a program. It does not depend on how the member pays.
:::

## Manage attendance from the member profile

From a member's profile, you can:

1. [View attendance history](/members/attendance/view-member-attendance/).
2. [Add attendance manually](/members/attendance/add-attendance-manually/).
3. [Delete an attendance entry](/members/attendance/delete-attendance/) if it was added by mistake.

## Choose the right way to record attendance

Use the member attendance area when you need to fix or add attendance for one person.

Use [Take Attendance](/schedules/taking-attendance/) when you are working from a scheduled class and want to record the whole session.

:::tip
If a member seems ready for their next level, check their attendance together with their current [membership](/members/memberships/) and your [Promotions report](/programs/levels/promotions-report/).
:::
