---
title: View a Member's Invoices
description: Find invoices and review billing details from a member's profile.
---

Use a member's invoice list to review what they were billed for, how much is still due, and whether a payment was attempted. Invoices are different from subscriptions: an invoice records a specific amount, while a subscription records a billing arrangement.

## Find a member's invoices

1. Open the member's profile.
2. Click the **Invoices** tab.
3. Open the invoice you want to review.
4. Check the invoice status, due amount, line items, and Charges section.

The profile's **Charges** tab lists payment attempts and recorded payments. Use the invoice for
the amount billed and the charge history for what happened when payment was collected. The
**Invoices** tab requires **View Billing** and the Billing feature to be enabled.

## Understand invoice status

| Status | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | The invoice has not been opened for payment. Review it before opening. |
| **Open** | The invoice is available for payment and may have a balance due. |
| **Partially Paid** | Some payment has been recorded, but a balance remains. |
| **Paid** | The invoice has been paid. Check the charges for payment details. |
| **Void** | The invoice was cancelled without collecting its remaining balance. |
| **Refunded** | Payment was refunded. Review the charge and refund status for details. |

An open invoice does not prove that a payment is currently processing. Check its Charges section before starting another attempt. A payment link may also remain pending while the member completes payment.

## Create or resolve an invoice

- To bill the member for a new one-time item, use [Create an Invoice](/billing/invoices/create-invoice/).
- To collect an amount due, see [Collect a Payment](/billing/invoices/collect-payment/).
- To return money or cancel an unpaid invoice, see [Refund or Void a Payment](/billing/invoices/refunds-and-voids/).
- To review payments across all invoices for the member, see [Review a Member's Charges](/members/billing/view-charges/).

Members can view non-draft invoices and receipts through [self-service invoices](/member-portal/view-and-pay-invoices/). For the school-wide invoice list and invoice concepts, see [Invoices](/billing/invoices/).
