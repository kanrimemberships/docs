---
title: Member Billing
description: Review and manage invoices, charges, subscriptions, payments, and payment methods for a member.
---

The **Billing** tab on a member's profile brings together the billing records for that person. The available cards depend on your permissions and whether your school has connected Stripe.

| Area | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Subscriptions** | Review recurring or fixed-term billing. |
| **Payments** | Review payments scheduled for a subscription. |
| **Invoices** | Review invoices or create a new invoice for the member. |
| **Charges** | Review charges across invoices or create a one-time charge. |
| **Discounts** | Manage member discounts that can be applied when creating an invoice. |
| **Payment Methods** | Add and manage saved payment details when Stripe is connected. |

Memberships and subscriptions are separate records. A subscription explains how the member pays; a membership records which program they are enrolled in.
