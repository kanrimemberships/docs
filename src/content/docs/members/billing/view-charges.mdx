## Planned topics

- Open the member's **Billing** tab and select **Charges**.
- Review charges across the member's invoices and identify failed attempts.
- Create a one-time charge when the member owes an amount outside a subscription.
- Follow an invoice to its charge, payment receipt, or refund details.
- Understand the difference between an invoice and a charge.

See [Invoices](/billing/invoices/) for school-wide invoice and charge management.
