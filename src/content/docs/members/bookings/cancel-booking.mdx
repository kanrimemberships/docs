Cancel a booking when a member no longer needs their spot.

## Cancel a booking

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Open the **Bookings** tab.
3. Open the booking you want to cancel.
4. Click **Cancel Booking** and confirm.

The booking remains in the member's history with a cancelled status. If the member still wants to attend, [book them into another session](/members/bookings/book-member-into-class/).

:::note
Cancel a booking when the member gives up the spot. Use **No-show** when they kept the booking but did not attend.
:::
