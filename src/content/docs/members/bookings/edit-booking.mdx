---
title: Edit Booking Notes
description: Update the staff notes on a member's booking without changing the reserved session.
---

Use the booking note to leave context for staff, such as a reminder about equipment or an arrangement with the member. Notes are separate from the booked class, date, and time.

You need the **Manage Bookings** permission, and the Bookings feature must be available to your school.

## Edit the note

1. Open **Members** and select the member.
2. Open **Bookings** and select the booking.
3. Choose **Edit Booking**.
4. Update the **Notes** field.
5. Select **Save Notes**.
6. Return to the booking and confirm the note is correct.

Keep notes brief and appropriate for staff to use when managing the booking. To change the reserved session, [reschedule the booking](/members/bookings/reschedule-booking/). To remove the reservation, [cancel it](/members/bookings/cancel-booking/).
