## Planned topics

- Open a confirmed booking from the member's **Bookings** tab.
- Choose another available session in the booking calendar.
- Understand that cancelled and completed bookings cannot be rescheduled.
- Check session capacity and booking settings before saving.

See [Member Bookings](/members/bookings/) for the other ways to manage a member's bookings.
