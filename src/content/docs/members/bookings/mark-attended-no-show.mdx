After class, update the booking so the member's record shows what happened.

## Mark a booking as attended

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Open the **Bookings** tab and select the booking.
3. Click **Mark Attended**.

## Mark a booking as no-show

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Open the **Bookings** tab and select the booking.
3. Click **Mark No-show**.

These actions do not require extra form details. For a session-wide workflow, see [Take Attendance](/schedules/taking-attendance/).
