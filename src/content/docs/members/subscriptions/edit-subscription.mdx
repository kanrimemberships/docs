Edit a subscription when the member's billing details change.

## What you can change

Depending on the subscription, you can update:

- the attached payment method
- the next payment date
- the payment type used for your records
- the selected price
- the payment amount override
- the payment interval and interval count overrides
- admin fees
- the end date, if the subscription is already fixed-term

## Edit a subscription

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Open the subscription.
4. Click **Edit**.
5. Update the fields you need.
6. Click **Save**.

## Important rules

- You can change or remove the attached payment method.
- You can only edit **End Date** on subscriptions that already have an end date.
- You cannot add an end date to an ongoing subscription from the edit screen.

:::note
If you remove the payment method, the subscription can stay on file. It simply will not have an attached method for automatic collection.
:::

:::tip
If the member is switching to a different payer, add the new payer's [payment method](/members/subscriptions/add-payment-method/) and select it on the subscription.
:::
