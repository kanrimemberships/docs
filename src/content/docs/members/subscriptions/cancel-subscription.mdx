Cancel a subscription when future billing should stop.

## Understand what cancelling does

Cancelling a subscription sets a **cancellation date**. The subscription stays active until that date arrives.

This is useful when a member has already paid through the end of the current period.

## Cancel a subscription

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Open the subscription.
4. Click **Cancel**.
5. Enter the **Cancellation Date**.
6. Enter a **Cancellation Reason** if you want to keep one on file.
7. Click **Cancel**.

:::note
You cannot cancel a subscription that is already cancelled or archived.
:::

:::caution
Cancelling a subscription does not end the member's [membership](/members/memberships/). If the member is also leaving the program, update both records.
:::
