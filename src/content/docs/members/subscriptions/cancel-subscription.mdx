Cancel a subscription when future billing should stop.

## Understand what cancelling does

Cancelling a subscription sets a **Cancellation Date**. A future date leaves the subscription in **Cancelling** status until that date; it becomes **Cancelled** on that date. Scheduled payments due on or after the date are cancelled. Leave the date blank to cancel immediately.

This is useful when a member has already paid through the end of the current period.

## Cancel a subscription

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Open the subscription.
4. Select the cancellation action on the subscription.
5. Enter a future **Cancellation Date**, or leave it blank to cancel immediately.
6. Enter a **Cancellation Reason** if you want to keep one on file.
7. Select **Cancel Subscription**.

:::note
If a cancellation is already scheduled, open the cancellation form to change its date or reason. Clear the date and save to uncancel the subscription and reactivate future scheduled payments. Archived subscriptions cannot be changed through this form.
:::

:::caution
Cancelling a subscription does not end the member's [membership](/members/memberships/). If the member is also leaving the program, update both records.
:::
