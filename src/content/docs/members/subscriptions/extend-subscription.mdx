Extend a subscription when a fixed-term plan should continue for longer.

## Before you start

You can only extend a subscription when:

1. it is a fixed-term subscription with an end date
2. it is not already cancelled

## Extend a subscription

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Open the subscription.
4. Click **Extend**.
5. Enter the date the extended term should begin.
6. Click **Extend**.

Kanri uses that date to create the next part of the subscription and move the end date further into the future.

:::note
Ongoing subscriptions do not need to be extended. They continue until you [cancel the subscription](/members/subscriptions/cancel-subscription/).
:::

:::caution
You cannot extend a cancelled subscription. If the member should continue paying, create a new plan arrangement instead of relying on a cancelled subscription.
:::
