---
title: Session Credits
description: Review and adjust class credits for a member's session-block subscription.
---

Session credits are prepaid visits attached to a member's **Session Block** subscription. They are not a general member balance and do not apply to a **Per Session** subscription.

## Review a credit balance

1. Open the member's subscription.
2. Confirm that its price type is **Session Block**.
3. Find **Session Credits** to review the credit blocks, remaining sessions, expiry dates, and usage history.

Each credit block records its total sessions and remaining sessions. For example, a 10-session block with 3 visits used should show 7 remaining.

## When attendance uses a credit

When staff record attendance, **Bill Session Usage** controls whether that attendance is billed against an eligible subscription. For a **Session Block** subscription, Kanri deducts an available, unexpired credit and uses the oldest eligible block first. A block with no remaining sessions or an expired block is not available for attendance deduction.

Per-session billing is different: attendance creates usage for that subscription, which is billed immediately or in a cycle according to the price settings. See [Billing Plans](/billing/billing-plans/) for the difference between session blocks and per-session pricing.

## Grant a session block

1. Open the member's active or pending session-block subscription.
2. Select the grant icon in the **Session Credits** area. The form is titled **Grant Credits**.
3. Enter the number of sessions to grant.
4. Optionally enter an **Expiry Date**. The grant form does not include a notes field. An expired block is not available for attendance deduction.
5. Select **Grant Credits**, then confirm the new block appears in the credit list.

For example, enter `10` to grant a 10-session block. Check that the selected subscription is the one the member will use for attendance.

## Adjust a credit block

Use an adjustment when you need to correct one specific block:

1. Open the session block in the subscription's **Session Credits** area.
2. Select the edit action.
3. Enter a positive number to add sessions or a negative number to remove them.
4. Enter a **Reason** for the adjustment, then save.

Kanri will not let you reduce a block below zero remaining sessions. For example, enter `5` to grant five sessions or `-2` to remove two unused sessions.

## Revoke credits across the subscription

Use **Revoke Credits** when you need to remove unused credits across blocks and do not need to choose an individual block. Enter **Sessions To Revoke** and a **Reason**. Kanri removes them from the oldest blocks first and does not allow revoking more than the remaining balance.

Review the credit and usage history after making a change. Credit adjustments do not refund an invoice; manage any money returned separately through the invoice's charge history.
