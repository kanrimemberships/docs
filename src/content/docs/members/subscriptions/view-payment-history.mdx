Use payment history to review what has happened on a subscription.

## What this page shows

The subscription payment list shows scheduled billing records, not necessarily completed payment attempts:

- **Upcoming** means the scheduled payment has not yet been invoiced.
- **Invoiced** means Kanri created an invoice for the scheduled payment; it does not mean payment succeeded.
- **Cancelled** means the scheduled payment was cancelled before invoicing.

Open an **Invoiced** scheduled payment to review its invoice and charge attempts. Use the invoice's **Charges** section to determine whether an attempt is processing, succeeded, failed, or disputed. Cancelled schedule records are not charge attempts.

## Open a subscription's payment history

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Open the subscription.
4. Review the payments section.
5. Open a payment to see more detail.

## Payment statuses you may see

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Upcoming | Scheduled but not yet invoiced. |
| Invoiced | An invoice was created for this scheduled payment. Check its charge history for the payment result. |
| Cancelled | The scheduled payment was cancelled before invoicing. |

Charge statuses are shown on the linked invoice or its **Charges** section. They include **Open**, **Processing**, **Success**, **Failure**, **Dispute Open**, **Dispute Won**, **Dispute Lost**, and **Dispute Warning**.

## Use payment history to answer common questions

Payment history can help you check:

1. when the next payment is due
2. whether the last payment succeeded
3. whether a failed payment should be retried
4. whether a member needs billing follow-up

:::tip
If you see a failed payment and the subscription still has a valid payment method, use [Retry Failed Payment](/members/subscriptions/retry-failed-payment/).
:::
