Use payment history to review what has happened on a subscription.

## What this page shows

Kanri shows two groups of payments for a subscription:

- **Open payments**, which are upcoming or still pending, up to 5 items
- **Past payments**, which are the most recent completed or failed payments, up to 5 items

Past payments do not include cancelled items in this summary.

## Open a subscription's payment history

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Open the subscription.
4. Review the payments section.
5. Open a payment to see more detail.

## Payment statuses you may see

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Upcoming or waiting to be processed. |
| Cancelled | The payment was cancelled before processing. |
| Processing | The payment attempt is in progress. |
| Success | The payment went through. |
| Failure | The payment failed. |
| Disputed Open | A dispute has been opened. |
| Disputed Won | The dispute was won. |
| Disputed Lost | The dispute was lost. |
| Disputed Warning | Stripe has warned of a likely dispute. |

## Use payment history to answer common questions

Payment history can help you check:

1. when the next payment is due
2. whether the last payment succeeded
3. whether a failed payment should be retried
4. whether a member needs billing follow-up

:::tip
If you see a failed payment and the subscription still has a valid payment method, use [Retry Failed Payment](/members/subscriptions/retry-failed-payment/).
:::
