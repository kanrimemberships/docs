A [subscription](/reference/glossary/) is the member's billing record.

It answers the question: **How and when does this member pay?**

This is different from a [membership](/members/memberships/), which answers: **Which program is this member enrolled in?**

## Understand what a subscription includes

A subscription can store:

- the billing plan and price being used
- the payment type used for the school's records, such as card, bank, cash, or scholarship
- the next payment date
- an optional saved payment method for automatic collection
- current billing status and payment history

## Subscription statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | The subscription starts in the future. |
| Active | The subscription is currently in effect. |
| Expired | A fixed-term subscription has ended. |
| Cancelling | The subscription has a future cancellation date. |
| Cancelled | The cancellation date has passed. |
| Archived | The subscription has been archived. |

Kanri only attempts automatic collection when the subscription has an eligible saved payment method. You can also keep subscriptions on file for payments handled outside Kanri.

:::note
A member can have a subscription without a membership, or a membership without a subscription. These records are independent.
:::
