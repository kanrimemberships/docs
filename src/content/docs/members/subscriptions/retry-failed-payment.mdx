Retry a payment when a subscription payment has failed and you want to charge again right away.

## Before you start

Make sure:

1. the payment you are looking at has failed
2. the subscription has an attached payment method

## Retry a failed payment

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Open the subscription.
4. Open the failed payment in the payment history.
5. Click **Retry**.
6. Confirm the retry.

Kanri creates a new charge attempt and charges the attached payment method immediately.

:::note
Retry uses the payment method currently attached to the subscription.
:::

:::caution
If the subscription does not have a payment method attached, you cannot retry the charge. Add a payment method first, then try again.
:::
