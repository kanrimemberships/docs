---
title: Contact Of
description: See which members are linked to a person as their contact.
---

The **Contact Of** tab lists the member profiles connected to this person. Each row shows the member's name, the relationship, and whether this person can manage them.

Use this tab when you need to check who a parent, guardian, or other contact is linked to. Select a member's name to open that profile.
