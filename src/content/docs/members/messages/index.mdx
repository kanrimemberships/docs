Every member's profile has its own **Messages** tab. It's the same [Inbox](/messages/) as the
main **Messages** area in the sidebar, filtered down to just this member and their
[contacts](/members/contacts/), so you can see and continue a conversation without leaving their
profile.

## What you can do here

- See every conversation with this member or their contacts, in one place
- [Send a new message](/members/messages/send-message-to-member/) to the member or a contact
- Reply, mark read/unread, flag, or archive a conversation, exactly as you would in the main Inbox

For the full detail on any of these actions, see
[Reply & Manage Conversations](/messages/reply-and-manage-conversations/). Broadcasting to many
members at once isn't available from a member's profile. See
[Send a Broadcast](/messages/send-a-broadcast/).

:::tip
You can message any contact directly from this tab regardless of their settings. **Receive
Communications**, set on the contact's [edit page](/members/contacts/), only controls whether that
contact is automatically included when you later [broadcast](/messages/send-a-broadcast/) to this
member's whole program or group.
:::
