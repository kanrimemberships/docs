title: Member Messages

When your role has access to the member profile and **View Messages**, and the Communications Hub
feature is enabled, the profile shows a **Messages** tab. It's the same [Inbox](/messages/) as the
main **Messages** area in the sidebar, filtered to conversations with this member and their
[contacts](/members/contacts/), so you can see and continue a conversation without leaving their
profile.

You need **Manage Messages** to compose or send messages. Staff without that permission can read
conversations, but other actions depend on their permissions.

## What you can do here

- See every conversation with this member or their contacts, in one place
- [Send a new message](/members/messages/send-message-to-member/) to the member or a contact
- Use the reply, read/unread, flag, and archive actions described in
  [Reply & Manage Conversations](/messages/reply-and-manage-conversations/), when your role allows them

For the full detail on any of these actions, see
[Reply & Manage Conversations](/messages/reply-and-manage-conversations/). Broadcasting to many
members at once isn't available from a member's profile. See
[Send a Broadcast](/messages/send-a-broadcast/).

:::tip
You can select a linked contact for an individual message from this tab even if **Receive
Communications** is off. For a program or group [broadcast](/messages/send-a-broadcast/), a linked
contact is included only when either **Receive Communications** or **Can Manage** is on. These
settings are separate from the permission needed to send messages.
:::
