Send a message from a member's profile when you want the conversation tied to that specific
person, without searching for them from the main Inbox.

## Before you start

The Communications Hub feature must be enabled. Your role needs access to the member profile and
**View Messages** to open the **Messages** tab, and **Manage Messages** to compose and send.

Make sure the member, or one of their [contacts](/members/contacts/), has at least one contact
method you can use, an email address for email, or a phone number for SMS.
SMS also depends on the school's messaging setup and availability. If SMS is unavailable or
suspended, use email or ask a school administrator to review the messaging settings.

## Send a message

1. Open the member from the [Members list](/members/).
2. Click the **Messages** tab.
3. Click **Compose**.
4. In the recipient selector, choose the member's or a linked contact's email address or phone
   number. You can also type an email address or phone number and press Enter.
5. Click **Templates** if you want to start from a saved [template](/messages/templates/), or
   write the message from scratch.
6. Enter a **Subject** when sending email. The subject field is not needed for SMS.
7. Write your message in the content editor.
8. Optionally, set **Send at** to schedule the message. The date and time use the school's time
   zone; leave the field blank to send immediately.
9. Click **Send** (or **Schedule**, if you set a send time).

The conversation appears both on this member's **Messages** tab and in the school-wide
[Inbox](/messages/).

:::tip
Type `{{` in the subject or content editor to insert a placeholder, like the member's first name.
See [Message Placeholders](/reference/message-placeholders/) for the full list.
:::

The **Receive Communications** contact setting does not prevent you from selecting that contact
for this individual message. It is used when linked contacts are included in program or group
broadcasts; those broadcasts include contacts whose **Receive Communications** or **Can Manage**
setting is on. A member-scoped message is not a broadcast.
