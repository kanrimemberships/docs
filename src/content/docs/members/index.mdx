A **member profile** holds details about a student or another person connected to your school. Use profiles for students, prospective students, parents, guardians, and other contacts. Staff accounts and permissions are managed separately.

Members are the centre of everything in Kanri. A member can have [memberships](/members/memberships/) (enrolment records), [subscriptions](/members/subscriptions/) (billing records), [attendance](/members/attendance/), [bookings](/members/bookings/), [notes](/members/notes/), [interactions](/members/interactions/), [documents](/members/documents/), and a [gallery](/members/gallery/).

## Member types

Every profile in the Members list has a **type** that describes their relationship to your school:

| Type        | Who it is                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Lead**    | A prospective student. You can choose this type when creating a profile. Lead-management tools require a Professional or Enterprise plan. |
| **Member**  | A student. Membership or subscription setup can also change a Lead, Contact, or Staff profile to Member. |
| **Contact** | A parent, guardian, or other person linked to a member. A Lead or Staff profile can become a Contact when you link them this way. |
| **Staff**   | A person with a staff role. Staff access and roles are managed separately from member profiles. |

Choose **Member**, **Lead**, or **Contact** when creating a profile. Kanri can update the type later when you give the person a staff role, link them as a contact, or add a membership or subscription.

The **Lead** option is available with a Professional or Enterprise plan.

:::note
Lead-management tools, including the public lead page and lead pipeline, require a Professional or Enterprise plan. [Compare plans →](/reference/plans/)
:::

## Profile fields

When you open a member's profile, you'll see the following information:

| Field                      | Description                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First name / Last name** | The member's full name. Both are required.                                                                                                                     |
| **Email**                  | Used for sending messages and for portal login. The same email cannot be saved on multiple profiles in your school. |
| **Phone**                  | Used for SMS messages. The same phone number cannot be saved on multiple profiles in your school. |
| **Birthday**               | Used in the [Birthday report](/reports/other/birthdays/) and displayed as an age on the profile.                                                               |
| **Gender**                 | Male, Female, or Other. Optional. |
| **Family Status**          | Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Separated, Common Law, or Other. Optional.                                                                                 |
| **Address**                | Street address, city, province/state, postal code, and country. Edited separately from other profile fields.                                                   |
| **Photo**                  | A headshot used for visual identification at the kiosk. Uploaded and cropped separately.                                                                       |
| **Member number**          | A unique number automatically assigned when the member is created. Members can use it or search by first name to sign in at the [kiosk](/kiosk/). |

## The members list

Go to **Members** in the main navigation to find student, lead, and contact profiles.

The list has separate **Active Members**, **All Members**, **Leads**, and **Contacts** tabs. **Active Members** includes members with an active membership. **All Members** includes member profiles with active or inactive memberships. Leads and contacts have their own tabs.

The list shows each member's name, last attendance date, and the active programs they're enrolled in. Click a member's name to open their profile.

You can [search and filter](/members/search-and-filter/) the list by name, number, age, phone number, and more.

## Member profile tabs

A member's profile is organised into tabs. Each tab shows a different aspect of the member's record:

| Tab               | What it shows                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Overview**      | Key information cards: personal details, address, memberships, subscriptions, contacts, notes, interactions, and more. Customisable per staff account. |
| **Contacts**      | People linked to this member (parents, guardians, spouses, etc.) and who can manage them.                                                              |
| **Contact Of**    | Members linked to this person as their contact, including each relationship and whether this person can manage them. |
| **Memberships**   | Enrolment records showing which programs the member is enrolled in, their level, and status.                                                           |
| **Billing**       | Links to subscriptions, payments, invoices, charges, discounts, and saved payment methods. |
| **Attendance**    | A log of every class session this member has attended.                                                                                                 |
| **Bookings**      | Upcoming and past class bookings for this member.                                                                                                      |
| **Messages**      | Email and SMS conversations with this member.                                                                                                          |
| **Interactions**  | Immutable log entries recording events, incidents, and calls.                                                                                          |
| **Notes**         | Editable staff notes (medical info, reminders, special arrangements).                                                                                  |
| **Gallery**       | Files and photos uploaded for this member.                                                                                                             |
| **Documents**      | [Documents](/members/documents/) sent to or stored for this member. Document Management requires a Professional or Enterprise plan. |
| **Evaluation**    | Evaluation forms and responses, when enabled for your school. |
| **Logs**          | A full audit trail of every change made to this member's record. Requires a Professional or Enterprise plan.                                           |

You can also manage a member's [discounts](/members/discounts/), [session credits](/members/subscriptions/session-credits/), and [evaluation](/members/evaluations/) from their record. See the [billing overview](/members/billing/) for the billing tools available from the profile.

## The member profile vs. a user account

A **member profile** is a record in your school's data. It holds training history, billing, and personal details.

A **user account** is a login credential. It lets someone access Kanri as staff or as a member through the portal.

These are two separate things. A member profile can exist without any user account. When you [invite a member to the portal](/members/invite-member-portal/), Kanri links their user account to their member profile.

If your school has **Auto-link accounts** turned on (in [Settings → Behaviors](/settings/behaviors/general-behaviors/)), Kanri automatically links a new user account to an existing member profile when the email addresses match.

## Member number

Every member is automatically assigned a unique member number when their profile is created.

Members can use this number or search by their first name to sign in at the [kiosk](/kiosk/). See [Member Number](/members/member-number/) for more detail.
