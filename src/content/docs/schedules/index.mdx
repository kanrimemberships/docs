---
title: Schedules and Bookings
description: Understand recurring schedules, individual class sessions, and member bookings.
---

Kanri uses three connected records to manage the timetable:

1. A **schedule** is a recurring timetable that contains one or more class entries.
2. A **session** is one occurrence of a class on a particular date and time.
3. A **booking** is a reservation for a session.

For example, a Monday 6 p.m. Karate Fundamentals entry in a schedule produces individual Monday sessions. A member can book a specific session when booking is enabled.

## Set up the timetable

1. Create a [program](/programs/create-program/) and [class](/programs/classes/create-class/).
2. Create a [schedule](/schedules/create-schedule/).
3. Add the class, day, time, and location to the schedule.
4. Activate the schedule to create its sessions.
5. Review the current timetable and test booking availability if you use bookings.

An active schedule can be changed, but changes to its entries are not applied to sessions until you sync it. Review [Sync a Schedule](/schedules/sync-schedule/) before publishing a timetable change.

Select **Save** in the timetable editor before syncing. If you are preparing next term, use a separate schedule with its own effective and end dates instead of expecting an entry edit to preserve the old timetable until a future date.

## Change the timetable safely

- **Edit** the schedule name or its effective and end dates.
- **Edit entries** to change class, variant, time, location, or booking settings.
- **Sync** to apply changed entries to generated sessions.
- **Deactivate** to stop the schedule and cancel future confirmed bookings.

Kanri warns you when a date change or sync will cancel future confirmed bookings. Check the warning before confirming.

## Bookings

Booking availability depends on the class or session settings, whether the schedule is active, and the school's booking behavior. Staff can review an individual member's bookings from their profile. See [Member Bookings](/members/bookings/) and [Session Bookings](/schedules/bookings/).
