Kanri's kiosk is a shared check-in screen for a tablet or computer at your school. Members find their profile and check in for a class. A signed-in member or guardian can also use mobile check-in when your school enables it.

## What kiosk check-in does

The kiosk helps members record attendance for scheduled classes. Depending on your school's settings, trial visitors with a booking can also check in. The separate staff **Attendance** screen supports attendance review, photo updates, and level promotions when enabled.

Kiosk settings can affect who may check in. For example, your school can require an eligible subscription or restrict the programs available based on billing-plan access. Review [Kiosk Behaviors](/settings/behaviors/kiosk-behaviors/) and test the experience before using it at the front desk.

## Set up the shared device

1. Confirm your school has the **Kiosk** feature.
2. Sign in on the reception device and open **Kiosk**. You need permission to access it.
3. Choose **Open Sign In**, then confirm the sign-out prompt. Set up each device separately; the kiosk address alone does not grant access.
4. Put the browser in full-screen mode and secure the device so visitors cannot leave the kiosk or access staff pages.
5. Test a check-in and confirm the attendance appears in Kanri.

The device needs an internet connection. If it goes offline, use the staff attendance workflow until the device reconnects. See [Set Up a Kiosk Device](/kiosk/set-up-kiosk/).

## Choose the right check-in method

- **Shared kiosk**: use at reception or the training area when members check themselves in.
- **Mobile check-in**: members or guardians sign in to their own account and choose who is checking in. See [Mobile Check-In](/kiosk/mobile-check-in/).
- **Staff check-in**: choose **Open Attendance** from Kiosk to review classes and confirm pending sign-ins. See [Taking Attendance](/schedules/taking-attendance/).
