## Let visitors email themselves a waiver link

Visitors can request an intake waiver link from the sign-in kiosk and complete the form on their own device.

Before you turn this on, make sure your school has the **Kiosk** and **Documents** features enabled. You also need to enable front-desk intake and choose an adult waiver template in **Settings → Leads & Intake**. If minors use a different waiver, choose a minor waiver template too; otherwise Kanri uses the adult template. Learn more about [front-desk intake](/lead-management/front-desk-intake/) and [setting up the kiosk](/kiosk/set-up-kiosk/).

1. Confirm that the **Kiosk** and **Documents** features are enabled for your school.
2. Open **Settings → Behaviors** and find the **Kiosk** section.
3. Turn on **Let visitors email themselves a waiver link**.

When all prerequisites are met and the setting is on, the sign-in kiosk home screen shows **Email a Waiver Link**. Visitors enter their email address and tap **Send Waiver Link**. Kanri sends a link to that address; the visitor can open it on their device to complete the intake form and sign the waiver.

This setting controls the kiosk button only. It does not change the separate **Email a Waiver Link** option staff use from Documents.

## Review attendance confirmation

The same Kiosk section has separate switches for **Require staff to confirm sign-ins** and **Require staff to confirm mobile check-ins**. When enabled, the corresponding check-in stays pending until staff confirm it in the Attendance kiosk. Pending entries do not count toward attendance or use session credits yet.

Use **Enable member mobile check-in** to offer [check-in from members' own devices](/kiosk/mobile-check-in/). Turning on the shared kiosk's trial sign-in option does not by itself enable mobile check-in.
