---
title: Manage Member Kiosk Holds
description: Place a temporary check-in hold with a message, then clear it when resolved.
---

A kiosk hold prevents one member from signing in at the kiosk or through mobile check-in. The shared kiosk displays the message you enter; mobile check-in directs the member to the front desk. Use a hold when staff need to speak with the member before check-in, and remove it when the issue is resolved. You need permission to manage members and the Kiosk feature to place or clear holds.

## Place a hold

1. Open the member's profile.
2. Select **Place Hold** in the kiosk-hold area.
3. Enter a message of up to 500 characters, or choose a quick message. The message is required and appears on the shared kiosk.
4. Select **Place Hold**.
5. Confirm the profile shows an active hold.

For example: *Please see the front desk before signing in.* Avoid putting sensitive payment or personal details in a message visible on a shared screen.

## Clear a hold

1. Open the member's profile and find the active hold.
2. Select **Clear Hold** and confirm the prompt.
3. Confirm the hold is no longer active.
4. If the member is present, ask them to try checking in again.

Use a clear message and keep it current. A hold is an access control for kiosk check-in; it does not cancel a membership, subscription, or booking.

If the member still cannot check in after the hold is cleared, see [Why Can't a Member Check In?](/troubleshooting/member-cant-check-in/).
