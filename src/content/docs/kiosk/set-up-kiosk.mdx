---
title: Set Up a Kiosk Device
description: Open Kanri's check-in screen on a dedicated tablet or computer.
---

Use a dedicated internet-connected tablet or computer as the shared check-in screen. Keep the device on the kiosk page and prevent visitors from opening staff or browser settings.

## Open the kiosk

1. Confirm the school has the **Kiosk** feature enabled.
2. On the reception device, sign in as staff and open **Kiosk**. You need permission to access the kiosk.
3. Select **Open Sign In** for member self-check-in, or **Open Attendance** for the staff attendance screen.
4. Confirm the prompt that opening the kiosk signs you out for security. The kiosk is opened for this browser; copying its address to another device does not set that device up.
5. Put the browser in full-screen mode and keep the kiosk page open.
6. Confirm the device has stable Wi-Fi and is plugged in or charged.
7. Test a member check-in and check the member's attendance history afterward.

## Secure the shared device

Use the device's built-in guided-access, screen-pinning, or kiosk mode so a visitor cannot navigate to other websites or access another user's account. Keep the device in a visible, supervised area. Do not save staff passwords in a public browser.

## Test your school's check-in rules

Before opening the kiosk to members, review [Kiosk Behaviors](/settings/behaviors/kiosk-behaviors/). Check trial sign-in, subscription checks, program access, photo updates, and promotion behavior as relevant to your school. Test a normal member and a trial visitor so staff know what to expect.

Photo updates and promotions belong to the staff **Attendance** view when enabled, not the unattended member sign-in screen. If sign-ins require staff confirmation, use the Attendance view to confirm pending entries.

If the kiosk cannot load, check the device's internet connection. If its browser access has been cleared, sign in and open **Kiosk** again on that device. If a member cannot check in, see [Why Can't a Member Check In?](/troubleshooting/member-cant-check-in/) or [Manage Member Kiosk Holds](/kiosk/member-holds/).
