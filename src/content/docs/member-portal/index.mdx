## What this page will cover

- What the Member Portal is: a restricted view of Kanri that members can log into to see their own information
- What members can see: their profile, memberships, upcoming bookings, invoices and receipts, shared documents, and public gallery items
- How a parent or guardian with permission can see information for members they manage
- What members cannot do: use staff billing controls, change membership status, see unrelated members, or access school administration
- How member access is linked to their member profile (via email address matching, controlled by the Auto-link behavior)
- How to invite a member to log in (they need an invitation or to sign up via the Lead Page)
