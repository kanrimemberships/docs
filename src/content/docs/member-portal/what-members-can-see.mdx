---
title: What Members Can See
description: See which member and guardian self-service pages are available and how access is scoped.
---

Self-service pages use the member record linked to the signed-in account. For supported pages, a contact with **Can Manage** access may also see information for the linked member. This does not mean every page displays the same data or uses a profile picker.

## Memberships and bookings

- The home page shows active memberships for the signed-in member and members they manage. The **Memberships** page can include active and inactive memberships.
- The **Bookings** page shows upcoming and past or cancelled bookings for the signed-in member and managed members. Booking availability depends on the Bookings feature; booking a session requires an eligible active membership. Cancellation and rescheduling are subject to school settings and booking rules.
- The **Calendar** link appears when the Calendar feature is enabled.

The portal does not provide a general member-profile editing form. Ask school staff to correct a name, contact detail, or other profile information.

## Invoices and receipts

The invoice self-service list includes non-draft invoices for the signed-in member and any member records they can manage. When more than one member's invoices are listed, the invoice card identifies the member. Open an invoice to review it; successful charges and refunds have receipt details and downloads. This page does not collect a payment or change billing settings. See [View Invoices and Receipts](/member-portal/view-and-pay-invoices/).

## Documents and gallery

When Documents is enabled, the **My Documents** page lists documents for the signed-in member and managed members. When Gallery is enabled, a member or managing contact can view public items and folders in that member's gallery only when every parent folder is also public. Private gallery content requires staff with **View Gallery** permission.

## What remains staff-only

Members do not get the school's full member list, unrelated profiles, staff notes and interactions, draft invoices, staff payment-collection tools, or school administration settings. Staff manage profile and membership changes in the staff application. Do not share a staff login with a member.

## Guardian access

Guardian access is based on a linked contact relationship with **Can Manage** enabled, not simply on being listed as a contact or having **Receive Communications** enabled. Check the linked account and contact relationship if the guardian cannot see the member's memberships, bookings, invoices, or documents. See [Add a Contact](/members/contacts/add-contact/) and [Troubleshoot Member Portal Access](/troubleshooting/member-portal-access/).
