## What this page will cover

- The member's home/dashboard view
- Profile: viewing (but not fully editing) their own details
- Memberships: seeing which programs they're enrolled in and their current status
- Invoices: viewing non-draft invoices for themselves or members they manage
- Charges, refunds, and downloadable receipts
- Upcoming bookings: seeing what classes they're booked into
- Documents and gallery items shared with them
- What members cannot change, including staff billing and membership settings
- How to get something changed (contact the school)

Members and contacts with permission to manage a profile can review invoices and receipts through [self-service invoices](/member-portal/view-and-pay-invoices/). The self-service page does not provide staff billing controls.
