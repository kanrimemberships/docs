## How group membership works

A group is a named collection of people connected to your school. You can use it to organize members and target a [broadcast](/messages/send-a-broadcast/). Group assignments determine who is included. You can combine multiple assignment types in one group.

| Assignment type | Who it includes |
|---|---|
| **Individual members** | Specific profiles you select. They stay in the group until you remove the assignment. |
| **Program and level** | Members with an active membership in a selected program. You can limit the group to a level range. Membership updates change who matches. |
| **Custom filter** | Members whose profiles match conditions you choose in the filter builder. Kanri checks the filter against member records when resolving the group. |

If a person matches more than one assignment, they are still included only once.
