---
title: Add Members to a Group
description: Add specific member profiles to a group and review the resulting audience.
---

Use individual assignments when you want to select exact people, such as a tournament team or a one-time volunteer list.

## Add members

1. Open **Groups** and select the group.
2. Select **Assign**.
3. Choose **Individual Members**.
4. Search for one or more members and select each profile to include.
5. Submit the assignment.
6. Choose **Preview Members** on the group page and verify the list.

An individual assignment stays in the group until you remove that person's assignment. Removing someone from a group does not delete their member profile.

Removing the individual assignment is not an exclusion rule. If the person also matches a program
assignment or dynamic filter, they remain in the group. Adjust that rule and preview again.

## Example: parent meeting

Create a group called *New Family Orientation*, add the families attending the meeting, and preview the list. Send the time and location through a [group broadcast](/groups/send-broadcast-to-group/). If the group is only for one event, remove the assignments after the event or keep the group for follow-up messages.

For groups that should change as memberships change, use [Program & Level](/groups/add-program-to-group/) or a [Dynamic Filter](/groups/add-members-by-filter/) instead.
