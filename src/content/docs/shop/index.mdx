Shop helps you manage the products your school sells, from uniforms and belts to equipment and merchandise. You can track stock, order more from suppliers, and follow each sale from payment through pickup.

## How a product sale works

1. Add products and options in [Products and Variants](/shop/products/). Create [bundles](/shop/products/#create-a-product-bundle) when you sell several products together at a pack price.
2. Create an invoice for a member, or leave the member blank for a walk-in sale. Add products or custom line items. See [Create an Invoice](/billing/invoices/create-invoice/).
3. Collect or record payment. Each attempt appears as a [charge](/members/billing/view-charges/).
4. If the items need to be handed over, use [Fulfill Product Sales](/shop/fulfil-sales/).
5. Use [Manage Inventory](/shop/inventory/) and [Shop Reports](/shop/reports/) to review stock and sales.

An invoice is the bill for the sale. A charge is one payment attempt against that invoice. One invoice can have multiple charges, such as when a payment fails and you try again.

### Example: selling a uniform that is out of stock

Suppose a member orders a size 2 uniform, but you have none available. If the product and school settings allow selling without stock, add the uniform variant to the member's invoice and take payment as usual. The invoice keeps the sale and the outstanding fulfillment together, even though there is no stock to hand over yet. If selling without stock is blocked, order the uniform first and invoice the member once you can sell it.

Create a purchase order for the missing size. When the delivery arrives, record the quantity received and allocate a unit to the waiting invoice. The member's order then appears in **Ready for Pickup**. When they collect it, record the handover there. This keeps three different events clear: the member paid, the supplier delivered stock, and the member collected the item.

:::tip
If the customer is standing at the counter, you can still create an invoice for a walk-in sale by leaving the member blank. If the item is handed over immediately, use **Fulfill Now** rather than reserving it for later pickup.
:::

## What Shop tracks

| Record | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Product and variant | What you sell, including the specific size or option, price, and supplier |
| Bundle | A saved pack of component products, with fixed variants or choices made when selling |
| Inventory | How many units are on hand, reserved, or still needed for sales |
| Purchase order | What you ordered from a supplier and what has arrived |
| Invoice | What the customer bought and the amount due |
| Charge | Whether a payment attempt succeeded, failed, or is still processing |
| Fulfillment | Whether the item is waiting for stock, reserved, or handed over |

These records connect, but they do not happen automatically as one step. For example, **Place Order** does not email the supplier, and a paid invoice does not always mean the item has been collected.
