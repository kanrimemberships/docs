Kanri's Documents feature helps you prepare forms, collect signatures, and keep a member's document history together. It is separate from the [member gallery](/members/gallery/), which stores photos and other files.

## How documents work

1. Create a [document template](/documents/templates/) for a form you use repeatedly.
2. Choose who signs it, whether a staff witness is required, and how long it remains valid.
3. Add the template content using the rich-text editor or a PDF overlay.
4. From a member profile, create a document from the template and send it for signing.
5. Review the document's status in **Documents** or on the member's **Documents** tab.

You can also [upload an already signed document](/documents/upload-and-manage/) to a member's record.

## Document statuses

The staff document list includes status filters such as **Awaiting Member Signature** and **Awaiting Staff Signature**. A member or guardian sees documents waiting for their signature, signed documents, and other completed or closed statuses in their self-service document list.

A member's signature completes a document only when no staff witness is required. Otherwise, it waits for the witness signature. A signer can decline a request, and staff can void a request that is still awaiting a member or witness. A signing deadline and a signed document's expiry date serve different purposes: one limits when it can be signed; the other records how long it remains valid.

## Waivers

Waivers are document templates used during enrolment or visitor intake. You can connect waiver templates to programs or configure one for [Front-Desk Intake](/lead-management/front-desk-intake/). See [Waivers](/documents/waivers/).
