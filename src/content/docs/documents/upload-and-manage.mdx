description: Add a pre-signed file to a member record and track documents sent from templates.

Use the member's Documents tab to upload a document that was signed outside Kanri or to review documents created from templates.

## Upload a pre-signed document

1. Open **Members** and select the member.
2. Open **Documents** and choose **Upload**.
3. Drag a file into the upload area or select it from your device. The upload form accepts PDF, image, Word, and other file types shown in the picker.
4. Enter a descriptive **Document name**.
5. Optionally add an expiry date.
6. Save the document.
7. Confirm it appears in the member's Documents list.

This upload flow stores a file on the member record. It does not collect a new signature. To request a signature in Kanri, [create a document from a template](/documents/request-signature/).

## Review documents sent from templates

Open **Documents** to browse the school's document list, or open a member's Documents tab to see their records. Use the status filters to find documents awaiting a member signature or staff witness signature. Open a record to review its status and available actions.

Use the record's **Preview** or download control when a file is available. For an uploaded document, downloading retrieves the original upload; it does not convert a Word document or image into a signed PDF. A completed signing process can also provide a **Certificate of Completion** when one has been generated.

Staff need **View Documents** to review and download records, and **Manage Documents** to upload or change them. Document files share the school's [storage allowance](/reference/plans/#storage) with other uploaded files.

## Keep records clear

Use names that identify the document and, when helpful, its date. For example, `Membership agreement - 2026` is easier to distinguish later than `scan.pdf`. Keep expiry dates accurate so staff can identify documents that need renewal.
