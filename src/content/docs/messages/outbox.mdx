The **Outbox** tab in **Messages** is a record of everything your school has sent, not just what
you've written by hand. Alongside messages staff composed from the [Inbox](/messages/), it
includes emails Kanri sends automatically, like payment receipts, staff invites, and
document-signing links, and every [broadcast](/messages/send-a-broadcast/) you've sent, with its
own delivery progress.

## Browse the Outbox

1. Go to **Messages** and open the **Outbox** tab.
2. Use the search box to find something by recipient.
3. Use the **All** / **Messages** / **System** filter to narrow the list: **Messages** shows only
   things staff composed, **System** shows only automatic emails.
4. Select an entry from the list to see it on the right.

A broadcast, or a bulk send to several individually-selected members, shows up as its own entry in
the list (labeled **Broadcast** or **Group**), alongside individual emails, rather than one row per
recipient.

:::note
Sensitive details inside an email, like a password reset link or a one-time PIN, are replaced with
a redacted placeholder in this view. This lets you review what a member received without being
able to use the link or code yourself.
:::

## Check a broadcast's progress

Selecting a broadcast or bulk-send entry shows:

- Its overall progress, how many recipients have been processed out of the total
- A preview of the message that was sent (placeholders show as their raw form, like
  `{{member.first_name}}`, not the substituted value, since a broadcast doesn't have one single
  recipient)
- A list of every recipient, their delivery status, and a link to that recipient's own copy of the
  message

Selecting **View details** from the send-progress modal right after sending a broadcast takes you
straight to that broadcast's entry here.

### Cancel a scheduled broadcast

If you scheduled a broadcast to send later, its entry shows **Scheduled for** the date and time you
chose, with a **Cancel Scheduled Broadcast** button. Cancelling stops it from sending, it's the
only way to stop a scheduled broadcast once you've confirmed the send.

## Delivery statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Queued | Waiting to be sent |
| Sending | Being sent right now |
| Sent | Handed off to the email or SMS provider |
| Delivered | Confirmed as delivered to the recipient's inbox or phone |
| Opened | The recipient opened the email |
| Failed | Sending failed, and won't be retried |
| Bounced | The recipient's email address rejected the message (email only) |
| Suppressed | Not sent, because that address has previously opted out or hard-bounced |
