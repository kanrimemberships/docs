Kanri's Messages area is where every email and text message to your members lives, whether it's a
one-to-one reply to a parent's question or a broadcast to your whole school. It has three tabs:
**Inbox**, **Outbox**, and **Templates**.

You'll find Messages in two places:

- **Messages** in the main sidebar — the school-wide inbox, with every conversation, the full
  Outbox, and all your Templates.
- The **Messages** tab on a member's profile — the same inbox, filtered down to just that member
  and their [contacts](/members/contacts/). Handy when you're already looking at someone's profile
  and want to see or continue a conversation with them.

## Conversations vs. broadcasts

These are the two ways messages get sent, and it's worth keeping them straight:

- A **conversation** is a one-to-one thread with a single member or [contact](/members/contacts/),
  by email or by text message (SMS). Conversations behave like an email inbox: you can reply, mark
  them read or unread, flag important ones, and archive old ones.
- A **broadcast** is one message sent out to many members at once, targeted at a
  [program](/programs/), a [group](/groups/), or a custom filter. Broadcasts don't create back-and-forth
  threads. They're for announcements, reminders, and newsletters.

See [Send a Message](/messages/send-a-message/) for conversations and
[Send a Broadcast](/messages/send-a-broadcast/) for broadcasts.

## The three tabs

- **Inbox** — every conversation with a member or contact. Search, filter, reply, flag, and
  archive. See [Send a Message](/messages/send-a-message/) and
  [Reply & Manage Conversations](/messages/reply-and-manage-conversations/).
- **Outbox** — every email your school has ever sent, including automatic emails like receipts and
  invites, plus a dedicated view of past broadcasts and their delivery progress. See
  [The Outbox](/messages/outbox/).
- **Templates** — reusable subject/body content you can drop into a message instead of writing it
  from scratch every time, including a drag-and-drop email builder. See
  [Templates](/messages/templates/).

## What you need to send messages

- **Email** works out of the box for every school.
- **SMS (text messages)** requires a phone number set up under
  [Settings → Branding → Phone Number](/settings/branding/phone-number/). Until that's done, the
  SMS option won't be available when composing.
- You'll need the **View Messages** permission to read conversations, and **Manage Messages** to
  send, compose broadcasts, or manage templates. See your school's [Roles](/settings/roles/) to
  check or grant these.
