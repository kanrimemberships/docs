---

A broadcast sends the same message to many members at once, unlike a [conversation](/messages/),
which is a one-to-one thread. Use a broadcast for announcements, reminders, and newsletters where
you don't need individual replies threaded back to you.

## Before you start

- Broadcasts are either **Email** or **SMS**, not both in the same send. If you want to reach
  members by both, send two broadcasts.
- SMS requires a phone number set up under
  [Settings → Branding → Phone Number](/settings/branding/phone-number/).
- If you're using the [Email Builder](/messages/templates/build-with-email-builder/) for the
  content, the broadcast must be Email. Email Builder content can't be sent as SMS.

## Send a broadcast

1. Go to **Messages** and click **Compose**.
2. Switch the recipient mode from **Individual** to **Broadcast**.
3. Choose **Email** or **SMS** for this broadcast.
4. Search the **To** box and add one or more **programs** and/or **groups** to target. See
   "Choose who to target" below for what each option includes.
5. Click **Templates** to start from a saved [template](/messages/templates/), or write the
   message from scratch.
6. Enter a **Subject** if you're sending by email.
7. Write your message in the content editor.
8. Optionally, set a date and time to send the broadcast later instead of right away.
9. Click **Send** (or **Schedule**, if you set a send time).

Kanri shows a progress modal while the broadcast goes out, since sending to a large number of
members happens in the background rather than all at once. You can dismiss it and keep working;
click **View details** to jump straight to the broadcast's entry in the [Outbox](/messages/outbox/).

:::tip
Changed your mind about a broadcast you scheduled for later? You can cancel it from the Outbox
right up until it sends. See [Cancel a scheduled broadcast](/messages/outbox/#cancel-a-scheduled-broadcast).
:::

## Choose who to target

You can combine any number of these in a single broadcast:

- **A program** — every member with an active [membership](/members/memberships/) in that
  program.
- **A group** — every member in that [group](/groups/), whether they were added directly or
  through a program assignment (with any level range the group specifies).
- **All members** — every active member at your school.
- **Lead programs** *(requires [Lead Management](/lead-management/))* — every
  [lead](/lead-management/) whose enquiry lists that program.

:::note[Professional & Enterprise]
Broadcasting to leads by lead program requires the Lead Management feature, available on
Professional and Enterprise plans. [Compare plans →](/reference/plans/)
:::

### Broadcasting to a custom filter

If programs and groups aren't specific enough, click **Broadcast by filter…** in the To box
instead. This replaces the program/group search with a filter builder, the same one used for
[custom reports](/reports/), so you can target members by any combination of fields, such as
membership status, level, or last attendance date. A filter is evaluated at send time and can't be
combined with programs, groups, or lead programs in the same broadcast.

## Placeholders still work

Broadcasts support the same placeholders as a one-to-one message, like the recipient's first name.
Because every broadcast recipient is a real member, there's no risk of an unresolved placeholder.
See [Message Placeholders](/reference/message-placeholders/) for the full list.

## Where broadcasts show up afterward

Every broadcast you send appears in the **Outbox** tab, under the **Broadcasts** view, with its
overall progress and a per-recipient delivery status. See [The Outbox](/messages/outbox/) for
details.
