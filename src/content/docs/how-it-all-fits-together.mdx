This is the most important page to read before anything else. Kanri has a handful of concepts that sound similar but mean very different things. Understanding how they connect will make everything else click.

## Memberships vs Subscriptions

The most common source of confusion in Kanri is the difference between a **Membership** and a **Subscription**. They are two completely separate records.

A **Membership** is the enrolment record. It answers: *which program is this member in, and what is their current status?* When you enrol a new student in Adult Karate, you're creating a Membership.

A **Subscription** is the billing record. It answers: *how much does this member pay, and when does the charge go out?* When you set up automatic monthly billing for that student, you're creating a Subscription.

A member can have a Membership without a Subscription. A cash-paying member or someone training for free is a common example. A member can also have a Subscription without a Membership, which is usually a data error worth fixing. The [Only Memberships](/reports/memberships/only-memberships/) and [Only Subscriptions](/reports/memberships/only-subscriptions/) reports exist specifically to help you find these mismatches.

:::tip
When something looks wrong with a member's account, always check both records separately. The problem is usually in one but not the other.
:::

## Programs, Classes, and Schedules

A **Program** is the top-level activity you offer: "Adult Karate", "Junior Ballet", "Swim Level 1". Members are enrolled into programs via Memberships.

Each program contains **Classes**, which are the named types of training within it. "Adult Karate" might have "Karate (White–Blue Belt)" and "Karate (Blue–Black Belt)" as separate classes. Each class has a "counts towards attendance" setting. If you turn it off, attending that class won't count toward a member's level promotion requirements.

Classes can optionally have **Variants**, which are sub-types that distinguish the format of a session without creating a whole new class. "Karate (Blue–Black Belt)" might have a "Sparring" variant and a "Kata" variant. Variants are optional; a class with no variants just runs as itself.

A **Schedule** is a named recurring timetable containing one or more class entries. Each entry defines a class, day, and time. **Sessions** are the occurrences members book and attend. When you take attendance, you're marking members as present for a session on a particular date. If you change an active timetable, [sync it](/schedules/sync-schedule/) to apply the changes. Review affected bookings before confirming; timetable changes are not just visual changes.

## Billing Plans and Prices

A **Billing Plan** is a named payment product: "Monthly Karate", "Annual All-Access", "Drop-In Pass". Think of it as a product on your price list.

Each plan has one or more **Prices**, which are the actual amounts and billing intervals. "Monthly Karate" might have an Adult price of C$120/month and a Junior price of C$80/month. Whether plans can have multiple prices is controlled by a school-level setting. See [Subscription Behaviors](/settings/behaviors/subscription-behaviors/) for details.

A **Subscription** links a specific member to a specific price on a plan. If it has a saved payment method, Kanri can charge the member on the schedule shown. Payments and attempts appear in the member's [payment history](/members/subscriptions/view-payment-history/). You can also record subscriptions paid outside Kanri without attaching a payment method.

## Levels and Promotions

Programs can optionally have **Levels**, which are a ranking system like belt grades or skill tiers. When a member is enrolled in a program they're placed at a starting level. Each level can have requirements: a minimum number of classes attended and a minimum number of days since their last promotion.

The [Promotions report](/reports/memberships/promotions/) helps you find members ready to advance. When you [promote a member](/members/memberships/promote-level/), Kanri updates their level and last-promotion date. Attendance after that date contributes to the next promotion; earlier attendance remains in their history.

If promotions happen at an assessment event, use a
[grading](/programs/levels/gradings/). A grading can cover one day or a window
of several days. Staff check eligibility and record promotions on a date
inside that window.

When changing your rules, a program can
[keep existing members' requirements until their next promotion](/programs/edit-program/#keep-level-requirements-until-next-promotion).
This lets new arrivals follow new rules without changing the targets for
members already training at the level.

:::note
"Promotions" in Kanri always means advancing a member's rank. It does not refer to discounts or marketing offers.
:::

## Groups

**Groups** are named collections of members. You can add individual members directly, or attach a whole program so that its active members are included. Use groups to organize people and to target [messages](/messages/send-a-broadcast/).

## A real example

You run Springfield Karate. A new student, Alex, walks in. Here's what you'd do:

1. [Add Alex as a member](/members/add-member/): enter their name, contact details, and emergency contact.
2. [Assign a Membership](/members/memberships/assign-membership/): enrol Alex in "Adult Karate" at White Belt, with a status of Active.
3. If Alex is paying by card or bank account through Kanri, [add a payment method](/members/subscriptions/add-payment-method/). For cash or other offline payments, you can skip this.
4. [Create a Subscription](/members/subscriptions/create-subscription/): link Alex to "Monthly Karate – Adult C$120/month". If the subscription has a payment method, Kanri can collect scheduled payments automatically.
5. Alex shows up for class and [checks in at the kiosk](/kiosk/), or a staff member [takes attendance](/schedules/taking-attendance/). Attendance is recorded against their Membership.
6. After enough classes, Alex appears in the Promotions report and is ready to move to Yellow Belt.

That's the core loop: **enrolment → billing → attendance → promotion**.

## Quick reference

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Program** | The activity you offer (e.g. "Adult Karate") |
| **Class** | A type of training within a program |
| **Class Variant** | An optional sub-format of a class (e.g. "Sparring") |
| **Session** | A single timed occurrence of a class, generated by a Schedule |
| **Schedule** | A recurring timetable entry that generates Sessions |
| **Membership** | A member's enrolment record in a program |
| **Level** | A rank within a program (e.g. "White Belt") |
| **Billing Plan** | A named payment product (e.g. "Monthly Karate") |
| **Price** | A specific amount and billing interval on a Plan |
| **Subscription** | A member's billing arrangement, which may be recurring, fixed-term, prepaid, or usage-based |
| **Group** | A named collection of members used for targeting |

For definitions of every term in Kanri, see the [Glossary](/reference/glossary/).
