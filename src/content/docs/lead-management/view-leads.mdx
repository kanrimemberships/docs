Leads are stored as member profiles in Kanri. Open the **Leads** tab on the [Members list](/members/) to view people who are currently leads. There is no separate lead pipeline screen.

## Finding your leads

1. Go to **Members** in the main navigation.
2. Select the **Leads** tab.

Use name, age, and **Interested in program** to narrow the list. Staff with full member-view
access also have **Created since**. Links from the Lead Funnel report can open more specific
cohorts, such as people with a booked or attended trial; these are not all separate controls
in the list's filter bar.

## Lead statuses

Each lead has a status that shows where they are in your follow-up process:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Just added — no one has followed up yet. |
| **Contacted** | Someone from your school has reached out. |
| **Qualified** | The lead is interested and likely to join. |
| **Trial** | A follow-up label you can select for someone trying your school. |
| **Converted** | A follow-up label you can select when someone joins. |
| **Lost** | No longer interested. |

The lead status is visible on the member's profile when the **Lead** section is shown. To update it, open the member's profile and click **Edit**.

These are follow-up labels, not an automatic record of bookings or payments. Creating a trial
subscription does not set Trial, and a profile's change from Lead to Member does not set Converted.

## Following up with a lead

You can contact a lead directly from their member profile:

- Click the member's name to open their profile.
- Go to the **Messages** tab to send an email or SMS.
- Use the **Notes** tab to record follow-up notes for your team.
- Use the **Interactions** tab to log calls, incidents, or other events.

## Converting a lead to a member

Creating a [membership](/members/memberships/) or [subscription](/members/subscriptions/) changes
the profile type from **Lead** to **Member** immediately. This is not conditional on payment,
an active membership status, or the subscription start date. Trial subscriptions also do this;
a trial class booking alone does not.

To convert a lead:

1. Open the lead's profile.
2. Go to the **Memberships** tab and [assign a membership](/members/memberships/assign-membership/).
3. If they are paying, go to the **Subscriptions** tab and [create a subscription](/members/subscriptions/create-subscription/).

When you give a lead a membership or subscription, their profile type changes to **Member** and they leave the Leads tab. The Lead Funnel report still includes converted people in its historical totals and drill-downs.

If someone disappears from Leads after you create a trial subscription, search the full Members
list before adding another profile. Also check for a partially completed Lead Page registration:
personal details are saved before the visitor finishes booking or signing.
