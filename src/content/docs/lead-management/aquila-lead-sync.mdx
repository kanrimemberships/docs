If your school previously used Aquila and migrated to Kanri, you can turn on **Aquila Lead Sync** to automatically pull newly-registered leads from Aquila into Kanri every few minutes — no need to rely on Zapier forwarding them.

## Before you start

You'll need the **Manage Settings** permission to change these settings. You'll also need your Aquila **Location ID** and **API Key** — the same credentials used during your original migration from Aquila.

## Turning on Aquila Lead Sync

1. Go to **Settings** in the main navigation.
2. Click the **Leads** tab.
3. Scroll to the **Aquila Lead Sync** section.
4. Enter your Aquila **Location ID** and **API Key**.
5. Toggle **Enable Aquila Lead Sync** to on.
6. Click **Save**.

Once enabled, Kanri checks Aquila about every five minutes. New profiles are created as
**Lead**, with status **New**. If the incoming details match an existing profile, Kanri can reuse
that profile and fill in missing details instead; its existing type and status are not reset.

## What counts as "new"

For registrations with a readable date, Kanri skips dates on or before the day you first enabled
sync. Aquila supplies a date rather than an exact registration time, so registrations later on
that same day are also skipped. Disabling and re-enabling sync does not move this original
starting date.

If a registration date is missing or unreadable, Kanri may process the record anyway. It checks
the newest 50 registrations per poll, not Aquila's entire history. This is a new-lead feed, not
a full migration or a guarantee that every older record will be imported.

Kanri tracks processed Aquila records and looks for existing profiles by email or phone, then
by lead name. Shared family details can affect matching, so review the result before creating a
second profile. A record already in Kanri may be supplemented rather than skipped entirely.

## Updating your credentials

To change your Location ID or API Key, go to **Settings → Leads → Aquila Lead Sync**, update the fields, and click **Save**. Leaving the API Key field blank keeps your currently saved key.

## Disabling Aquila Lead Sync

Toggle **Enable Aquila Lead Sync** off and click **Save**. Existing lead profiles already created in Kanri are not affected.
