Once your Lead Page is enabled, share its URL so prospective members can find it. Copy the
direct link from your school's settings rather than constructing an address yourself.

## Finding your Lead Page URL

1. Go to **Settings → Leads**.
2. Make sure **Enable Lead Page** is turned on.
3. Click **View** in the **Lead Page** section.
4. Copy the address from your browser.

The URL looks like:

```
https://app.kanrimemberships.com/lead/{your-school-id}
```

Copy this URL to share it.

:::tip
If you use a custom address, check your [vanity URL settings](/settings/branding/vanity-url/)
and test the actual link before printing it. Do not substitute an assumed hostname for the
address opened by **View**.
:::

## Where to share your Lead Page

**Your school website:** Add a "Sign Up" or "Book a Trial" button that links to your Lead Page. This is the most effective placement.

**Social media bio:** Paste the link in the bio section of your Instagram, Facebook, or other profile. Many schools use a link-in-bio tool (like Linktree) to combine multiple links — add your Lead Page there.

**Google Business Profile:** Add it as your "booking" or "appointment" URL so prospective members can sign up directly from Google search results.

**Email signature:** Include the link in your staff email signatures so it appears every time you send an email.

**In-person QR code:** In **Settings → Leads**, select **QR Code** in the **Lead Page** section to
download a code to print on posters or flyers. Visitors can scan it with their phone.
For visitors who are already at your front desk and only need registration and a waiver, use
the separate [Front-Desk Intake](/lead-management/front-desk-intake/) code instead.
