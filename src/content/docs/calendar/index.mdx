The **Calendar** brings school events and scheduled activity into one place. Depending on the calendars and layers you can view, it can show events, class sessions, bookings, and gradings. Use it to check what is happening on a date or to share an appropriate calendar feed with staff and families.

## Choose what to view

Use the calendar controls to switch between available calendars and layers. If you cannot see a calendar or event, check that it is enabled in the calendar view and that your role allows access.

Scheduled classes and their generated sessions are managed through [Schedules](/schedules/), not by creating a separate calendar event. Use a calendar event for activities such as a school closure, seminar, or tournament.

A calendar event is only a reminder. To stop check-ins or bookings during a closure, add a [closed day](/calendar/closed-days/) as well.

When the grading feature is enabled, scheduled gradings also appear in the calendar. Use the **Gradings** layer to show or hide them. Staff with program-view access can see gradings; members see gradings for their active programs. Grading details and rosters are staff-only. See [Manage Gradings](/programs/levels/gradings/).

Staff with permission to manage calendars can see all school calendars. Other users see public calendars and calendars shared with them. Staff with permission to manage schedules see the full schedule layer; members' sessions are filtered by their program enrolment and level. Booking access is separate from calendar access.

## Common tasks

- [Manage Calendars](/calendar/manage-calendars/): create calendars and control who can view them.
- [Create and Edit Events](/calendar/events/): add one-time or repeating school events.
- [Set Closed Days](/calendar/closed-days/): mark days when classes or bookings should not run.
- [School Closure Checklist](/calendar/school-closure-checklist/): review bookings and notify families when the school closes.
- [Share a Calendar Feed](/calendar/share-calendar-feed/): view eligible calendar events in another calendar app.

## Example: prepare for a tournament

Create a calendar event for the tournament with its date, start time, and location. If the event should appear on a shared calendar, choose the appropriate calendar and review its sharing settings. Keep regular class times in the schedule so attendance and booking behavior continue to work as expected.
