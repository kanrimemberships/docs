---
title: A Member Is Missing from Search or Reports
description: Check the member list, filters, and report settings when a person is not appearing.
---

First check which list you are searching. Kanri separates member profiles, prospective members, and people linked as contacts.

## Search the right list

1. Open **Members**.
2. Check **All Members**, **Leads**, and **Contacts**. A person may appear in a different tab than expected.
3. Search using a short part of the first or last name, or use the member number if you know it.
4. If you have **View Members (Full)** access, try searching by phone number.

Searches may not match the middle of a name or number. If searching `che` does not find someone named Chen, try `ch` or another starting character.

## Check filters and reports

Remove filters one at a time, then run the search again. On the Leads tab, check the program-interest filter if it is available.

Reports use their own date ranges, status filters, and access rules. Open the report and confirm its filters before concluding that the person is missing. For example, an inactive membership may not appear in a report focused on active memberships.

## If you still cannot find the record

Try a different spelling or the member number. Email is not a search field in the Members list. If no matching record appears, the profile may have been deleted. Deleted profiles do not appear in the Members list; contact support if you believe a record was deleted by mistake.
