title: Glossary
description: Plain-language explanations of common Kanri terms.
---

## A

**Active membership:** A member's current enrolment status for a program. It is separate from whether the member has an active subscription.

**Attendance:** A record that a member attended a particular class session. Staff can also mark a member as a no-show for a booking.

## B

**Billing plan:** A named group of prices used when setting up a member's subscription. See [Billing Plan Fields](/reference/billing-plan-fields/).

**Booking:** A reservation for a particular session. A booking is not the same as attendance.

**Broadcast:** One message sent to multiple recipients selected by a group, program, or filter.

**Bundle:** A saved pack of products sold together, such as a uniform and belts.
Its component products keep their own stock and fulfillment records. See
[Product Bundles](/shop/products/#create-a-product-bundle).

## C

**Class:** A kind of class offered by a school, such as Adult Karate or Junior Ballet. A schedule uses a class to generate sessions.

**Class variant:** An optional subtype of a class, such as Sparring or Kata.

**Contact:** A person linked to a member, such as a parent or guardian. A contact can have permission to manage the member's account.

**Conversation:** A message thread between staff and a member or contact.

## D

**Document template:** A reusable document that can be sent to members to read or sign.

## E

**Evaluation:** A form and response used to collect information during member onboarding.

## G

**Gallery:** A place to store and organize images or other files for the school or an individual member.

**Grading window:** The inclusive date range during which promotions can be
recorded for a grading. A window of zero extra days is a single-day grading.
See [Manage Gradings](/programs/levels/gradings/).

**Group:** A named collection of members used to organize people or target messages.

## I

**Interaction:** A dated record of an event, incident, call, or meeting related to a member.

## K

**Kept level requirements:** Earlier promotion rules and a next-level
destination preserved for a membership when the program's deferral setting is
on. They clear when the membership changes level. See
[Keep level requirements until next promotion](/programs/edit-program/#keep-level-requirements-until-next-promotion).

**Kiosk:** The check-in screen members or trial visitors use to sign in for a class.

**KPI:** A saved measure, such as a member or payment total, that can be displayed on the dashboard.

## L

**Lead:** A prospective member whose inquiry or trial progress is being tracked.

**Lead Page:** A public page where prospective members can send an inquiry and, when configured, book a trial or complete a waiver.

**Level:** A rank or stage within a program, such as White Belt or Yellow Belt.

## M

**Member:** A person with a profile in your school. A member can have memberships, subscriptions, bookings, attendance, documents, notes, and other related records.

**Member number:** A number assigned to a member profile. Members can use it to identify themselves at the kiosk.

**Member Portal:** The member-facing area where members can access information and actions made available by the school.

**Membership:** An enrolment record connecting a member to a program. It can include a status and level.

## N

**Note:** A staff-maintained note on a member's profile. Unlike an interaction, a note can be edited.

## O

**Outbox:** A record of outgoing messages, including messages sent by staff and some system-generated messages.

## P

**Price:** One billing option within a billing plan, such as an ongoing monthly price or a fixed-term option.

**Program:** A category of training offered by a school, such as Adult Karate or Junior Ballet.

**Promotion:** Moving a member to a higher level within a program.

## S

**Schedule:** A named timetable containing recurring class entries, with optional effective and end dates. Activating it makes its sessions available.

**Session:** One occurrence of a scheduled class at a particular date and time.

**Session block:** A price option that provides a set number of sessions, sometimes with automatic reload.

**Subscription:** A member's billing arrangement. It is separate from their program membership.

**Sync:** Applying schedule-template changes to an active timetable. Changes can reassign or cancel future bookings, so review the confirmation before syncing.
