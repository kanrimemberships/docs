---
title: Member Fields Reference
description: A plain-language guide to the information stored on member and lead profiles.
---

A member profile contains information about a person and their relationship with your school. Some fields are optional, and the fields you see can depend on the person's profile type and your role.

## Personal and contact details

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| First name and last name | The person's name. Both are required, with a maximum of 100 characters each. |
| Birthday | Date of birth. Kanri uses it for age-related views and birthday reports. It is also needed to choose the child or adult evaluation during onboarding. |
| Gender | Optional: Man, Woman, or Other. |
| Family status | Optional: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Separated, Common Law, or Other. Not shown in the contact's personal-details form. |
| Email and phone | Optional contact details used for communication. Email must have a valid format; phone numbers are checked using the school's country. An email or phone already used by another non-deleted profile at the school is rejected. Use Kanri's secure payment flow for payment methods, not these fields. |
| Address | Street, city, region, postal code, and country. Update the address separately from the other profile fields. |
| Photo | A profile image used to help identify the member. |
| Member number | A number Kanri assigns to the profile. Members can use it when signing in at the kiosk. |

## Lead information

Lead fields appear when they apply to a prospective member:

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| Lead status | The lead's current stage in your follow-up process. |
| Programs of interest | Programs the person is interested in. |
| Lead source | Free-text information about how the person found your school, such as `Community event`. Maximum 200 characters. |
| Further details | Additional information about the inquiry. Maximum 5,000 characters. |
| Referred by | Free-text name of the person who referred them, if applicable. |

Kanri does not provide a separate referral-type editor. Agree on consistent wording with your staff. See [Track Referral Sources](/settings/referral-types/).

## Related records are on other profile areas

A member's memberships, contacts, notes, attendance, bookings, documents, evaluations, messages, billing, and gallery items are managed in separate profile tabs or areas. They are not fields in the personal-details form.

- A [membership](/members/memberships/) records the program enrolment, status, and level.
- A [subscription](/members/subscriptions/) records a recurring or other billing arrangement.
- [Contacts](/members/contacts/) record a person's relationship to the member and whether they can manage the member's account.
