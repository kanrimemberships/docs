---
title: Timesheets
description: Review, correct, and export staff time records.
---

Managers can use **Timesheets** in the Shifts area to review clock records. The view includes filters for dates, staff, department, school where available, and whether a record is ongoing or complete.

## Find time records

1. Open **Shifts** and select **Timesheets**.
2. Choose a date preset or enter the **From** and **To** date and time.
3. Apply any available staff, department, school, or status filters.
4. Select **Refresh** to load the matching records.
5. Review the staff member, shift, department, clock-in and clock-out times, and duration.

An ongoing record has a clock-in time but no clock-out time yet. Check with the staff member before correcting it.

## Correct a time record

1. Find the record and select its **Edit** control.
2. Correct the clock-in or clock-out time.
3. Add a note explaining the correction.
4. Save the change.

Use the actual work times and leave a clear note so another manager can understand why the record changed. You can delete an incorrect clock record, but deletion cannot be undone.

Clock-out must be later than clock-in. Leaving clock-out blank makes the record ongoing again.

## Export the filtered records

1. Set the date range and filters you need.
2. Refresh the list.
3. Select **Download CSV**.
4. Open the downloaded file in your spreadsheet program.

The export follows the records currently shown. Clock-in and clock-out times use the time zone for each record's school, matching the times shown in the table. Records not tied to a school use the current Shifts view's time zone. Review the date range and filters before downloading it for payroll or another report.
