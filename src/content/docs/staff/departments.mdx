---
title: Staff Departments
description: Create departments and understand their role in shift planning and clock-in rules.
---

Departments give staff shifts a way to be grouped and filtered. A department can also allow its staff members to clock in without a matching scheduled shift when the school uses strict clock-in rules.

## Create or edit a department

1. Open **Settings → Departments**.
2. Select **New Department**, or open an existing department to edit it.
3. Enter a clear name, such as *Front Desk* or *Instructors*.
4. Decide whether to enable **Allow Free-Form Clock In**.
5. Save your changes.

The department form contains the department name and the free-form clock-in setting. Creating a department does not, by itself, add staff members to it.

The name is required and can contain up to 100 characters. Existing names cannot be reused, even with different capitalization. **Allow Free-Form Clock In** is off when you create a department unless you enable it.

## Understand free-form clock-in

When a school uses **Strict** clock-in mode, staff normally need a matching scheduled shift to clock in. Enabling **Allow Free-Form Clock In** for a department provides an exception for staff associated with that department. It does not switch the whole school to flexible clock-in.

Choose this setting only when the department needs that exception. For the school-wide mode and tolerance settings, see [Clock In and Out](/staff/time-tracking/).

## Use departments with shifts

Managers can select a department when scheduling a shift and filter the shift calendar by department. Use names consistently so staff and managers can find the right shifts quickly.
