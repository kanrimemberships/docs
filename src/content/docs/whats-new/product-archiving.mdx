## Improved

- **Product catalogue:** Archive products or individual variants to remove them
  from active selections without deleting their history. Archiving a product
  also archives its active variants; restoring it restores only those variants.
  Use **Show archived** to find archived catalogue items.
  [Manage products](/shop/products/#archive-products-and-variants).
