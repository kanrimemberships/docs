description: Product bundles, grading windows, and control over changes to level requirements.
---

## Added

- **Product options:** Define options such as size and color, then choose which
  combinations you sell. Each combination can have its own price, SKU, cost,
  supplier, and stock. [Set up product options](/shop/products/#set-up-sizes-colors-and-other-options).
- **Product bundles:** Create a saved kit from existing products, with fixed
  variants or choices made when selling. The invoice keeps component stock,
  tax, and fulfillment separate and adds a bundle savings discount when
  applicable. [Create a product bundle](/shop/products/#create-a-product-bundle).
- **Grading windows (Beta):** Allow promotions across several days, check
  eligibility on a chosen date, and choose whether unpromoted members roll
  over after the window closes. [Manage gradings](/programs/levels/gradings/).

## Improved

- **Keep level requirements until next promotion:** Enable this on a program
  before changing its levels to preserve existing members' requirements and
  next-level assignments until their level changes. Members reaching the
  level later use the updated rules. [Edit a program](/programs/edit-program/#keep-level-requirements-until-next-promotion).

## Fixed

- **Attendance and schedules:** Mobile check-in now respects attendance closures
  and class groups. Schedule sync reviews future confirmed bookings and cancels
  reservations that no longer fit the updated session.
  [Review schedule sync](/schedules/sync-schedule/).
- **Reports:** Booking CSV exports respect the selected month, Historical
  Memberships can include all descendant levels, and the Promotions cutoff
  uses the displayed promotion date. Absentees now focuses on active members,
  including those who have never attended after the selected absence period.
  [Use the Absentees report](/reports/attendance/absentees/).
- **Workflows (Beta):** Date-delay offsets are saved and applied, condition
  rechecks respect the trigger's direction, and **Add member** exposes its
  profile fields. The Birthday preset uses the correct field, but ordinary
  date triggers still do not repeat annually.
  [Review workflow steps](/workflows/steps/).
- **Gallery (Beta):** Folder privacy applies through every parent folder,
  including direct item and download access. Deep folder cleanup and
  same-folder duplicate-name checks are corrected.
  [Manage gallery sharing](/gallery/share-gallery/).
- **Staff shifts:** Disabling shift swapping blocks new offers and
  claims. Timesheet exports use the same school-timezone formatting as the
  table. [Review timesheets](/staff/timesheets/).
