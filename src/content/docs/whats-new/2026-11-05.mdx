## Improved

- **External assistant downloads:** Connected assistants can check student profile photos,
  search and count permitted school or member-gallery files, and download originals,
  including photos, videos and documents. Your existing permissions still apply, and
  download links expire after five minutes.
  [Get student photos](/settings/external-assistants/#get-student-photos) or
  [find gallery files](/settings/external-assistants/#find-and-download-gallery-files).
- **Product catalogue:** Archive products or individual variants to remove them
  from active selections without deleting their history. Archiving a product
  also archives its active variants; restoring it restores only those variants.
  Use **Show archived** to find archived catalogue items.
  [Manage products](/shop/products/#archive-products-and-variants).
- **Report and workflow field descriptions:** Field pickers now describe fields
  and related records to help you choose the right information.
  [Review report fields](/reports/custom-reports/record-field-reference/).
