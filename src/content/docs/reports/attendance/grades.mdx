---
title: Attendance Grades
description: Review members' A, B, or C attendance grades and recent changes.
---

When your school uses grades per program, attendance counts toward the program
saved when the member attended. Changing a membership's program does not move
earlier attendance into the new program. Trial attendance with a known program
counts toward that program too.

Attendance Grades shows the distribution of attendance grades and a table of
members whose grades changed. It is available only when the school has the
required attendance-grade behaviour enabled, along with Kiosk and Memberships.
Your report permission must also allow access.

## Read the grade summary

The summary groups eligible members into **A**, **B**, and **C** grades using
the attendance thresholds configured for your school. Grade windows and
thresholds come from the school's behaviour settings; they are not a universal
fixed definition. Ask an administrator which thresholds apply before
comparing your results with another school or period.

The changes table helps you spot members whose grade has recently moved. Use
the comparison selector to review changes over **1, 2, 4, or 8 weeks**. A
change is a useful reason to check in, but it does not explain the reason for
the change.

When grades are configured per program, attendance is attributed to the
program saved with the visit. Moving a membership later does not rewrite
earlier attendance. Trial attendance with a known program can count toward
that program as well.

## Example

If a member's grade has dropped over the selected comparison period, open
their record and review recent attendance and membership details before
contacting them. If the grade distribution seems unexpected, have an
administrator verify the school's grade thresholds and lookback settings.
