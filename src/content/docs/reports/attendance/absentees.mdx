---
title: Absentees Report
description: How to use the Absentees report to find members who haven't been attending recently.
---

The Absentees report helps you find members with an active membership who
have not attended recently. It also includes active members who have never
had confirmed attendance once they have been enrolled long enough.

## Choose the absence window

Choose one of the available windows: **14, 30, 60, 90, or 180 days**. The
default is 30 days. A member appears when their most recent confirmed attendance
was at least that many calendar days ago, using your school's timezone.

For members who have never had confirmed attendance, Kanri measures from the
creation date of their earliest currently active membership. A new active
membership does not restart this period if an older active membership still
exists. Members without an active membership are excluded, even if they have
old attendance. Unconfirmed or removed attendance does not reset the absence
period.

Use the window that matches your follow-up routine. A short window can help
catch missed classes early; a longer one can identify extended absences. The
report is a prompt for staff review, not a prediction that a member will
cancel.

## Review and follow up

1. Select the absence window.
2. Review the attendance dates. Members who have never attended have no **Last Attendance** date; check their membership creation dates.
3. Open the member's record and check their membership and recent activity.
4. Contact the member using your school's normal process, and record any
   follow-up as appropriate.

Before reaching out, check for a pause, schedule change, or other context that
could explain the gap. This report does not establish a reason for the
absence.
