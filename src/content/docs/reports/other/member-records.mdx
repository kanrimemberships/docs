---
title: Member Record Checks
description: Find missing contact details and review possible duplicate member records.
---

Use **Missing Contact Info** to find member records without a phone number or
email address. Choose whether to look for missing **Phone**, missing **Email**,
or **Either**. The default is Either, which includes a member missing at least
one of the two.

Kanri checks both the member and their active linked contacts. If a contact
already has the phone number or email address that the member lacks, the
member is not treated as missing that detail. This report does not identify
duplicates; use [Resolve](/reports/other/resolve/) for duplicate records,
shared details, and other contact-data cases.

## Review the list

1. Choose **Phone**, **Email**, or **Either**.
2. Review the matching member records and open each record to verify the
   details and linked contacts.
3. Update contact information using your school's normal process.
4. Export the filtered list as CSV if you need to coordinate cleanup.

Do not assume a missing value is an error. A person may intentionally have no
email address or phone number; check with the member or guardian before
changing the record.
