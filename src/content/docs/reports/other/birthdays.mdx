---
title: Birthdays Report
description: How to use the Birthdays report to find and reach out to members with upcoming birthdays.
---

The Birthdays report lists members with birthdays in a selected calendar
month. It defaults to the current month and includes members who have at least
one active membership. The table shows each member's name, birthday, and
current age, sorted by birthday.

## Choose a month

1. Open **Reports → Birthdays**.
2. Select a birth month. The default is the current month; choose another
   month to plan ahead or review birthdays from earlier in the year.
3. Open a member's record if you need to check details or follow your school's
   normal contact process.
4. Download the list as CSV if you need to coordinate birthday outreach.

This report selects a month of the year, not a rolling date window such as
“the next 30 days.” Review contact preferences and your school's
communications practices before sending a message. The report filters for
members with at least one membership whose status is marked active; it does
not include every person who has ever had a membership.

## Example

To prepare next month's birthday list, select that month and download the
CSV. Use the member links to confirm names and contact details, then coordinate
any outreach with your staff. If a person you expect is missing, verify that
their birthday is saved on the member record and that they have an active
membership.
