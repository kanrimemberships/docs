title: Health Report
description: A dashboard overview of your school's key metrics in one place.
---

Health is a school-level summary of current membership and subscription counts
plus selected activity during a reporting period. Choose **This Month**,
**This Quarter**, or **This Year**. The report opens on This Month and can be
downloaded as a PDF.

## Metrics on the page

The current-state cards show active members, active memberships, and active
subscriptions grouped by plan type: ongoing, fixed-term, paid-in-full, trial,
scholarship, session-block, and per-session. Ongoing and fixed-term
subscriptions are further split into free and non-free counts.

Period-based cards show:

- **New Members**: people whose first membership became active during the
  selected period.
- **Quits**: members with no active membership now and a membership that
  became inactive during the period.
- **Trial to Paid Conversions**: new paid subscriptions marked as conversions
  during the selected period.
- **Attendance**: unique members with confirmed attendance created during
  the period.

The current membership and subscription cards are snapshots for today; the
period selector applies to the activity cards. These figures do not represent
revenue, failed-payment totals, or a judgment about whether the school is
“healthy.” They are starting points for investigation, not targets or
benchmarks.

## Follow up on a number

Select a card to open the related member, membership, subscription, or
attendance records. For example, choose **Trial to Paid Conversions** and
review the linked subscriptions; use [Failing Subscriptions](/reports/billing/failing-subscriptions/)
to investigate current payment failures. To share the snapshot, select
**Download PDF**. The PDF includes the school's name and the report generation
date.
