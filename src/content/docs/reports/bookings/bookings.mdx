---
title: Bookings Report
description: How to view and export booking data across all schedules.
---

Booking Reports gives you a chart of booking activity over time, plus
summaries by program and class. It requires the Bookings feature and the
**View Bookings** permission.

## Set the period and grouping

The chart defaults to the last 90 days. Choose a 30-day, 90-day, 180-day, or
365-day period, and group the trend by day, week, or month. You can also
select a particular month as a spotlight period; within a spotlight month,
the chart groups by day or week.

The chart separates confirmed, attended, no-show, and cancelled bookings.
Summary figures also show total bookings and trial bookings. The program and
class summaries help identify where bookings occurred; the class summary is
limited to the 20 classes with the most bookings.

## Interpret the counts

These are booking counts, not unique people or a class-capacity/fill-rate
calculation. A single person can have multiple bookings. When comparing
periods, use the same date grouping and remember that a shorter or incomplete
period will generally have fewer bookings.

Select **Export CSV** to download booking records for the selected preset
time-range option. The export is a record-level list, while the chart is
grouped for trend review.

## Example

To compare weekly no-shows over the past six months, select the six-month
range and group by week, then review the no-show series. If a particular
class stands out, check the class summary and open the underlying booking
records before changing the schedule.
