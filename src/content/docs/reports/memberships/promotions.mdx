---
title: Promotions Report
description: How to use the Promotions report to find members who are ready to advance to the next level.
---

By default, the Promotions report lists active memberships that meet their
current level's date and confirmed-class requirements for advancing. Use
**Eligible by** for date-based planning, not as a guarantee that every listed
member has completed the required classes.

## Narrow the results

Filter by program, current level, next level, or **Eligible by** to focus the
list. Check the program's requirements and the membership's supporting
attendance or other criteria before promoting. Eligibility depends on the
requirements configured for that program.

**Eligible by** compares your selected date with the **Next Promotion Date**
shown in the report. It does not apply the minimum-class requirement, so
review the **Classes** column separately before promoting. Without a cutoff,
both date and class requirements must be met.

For levels tied to a [grading](/programs/levels/promotions-report/), the date
uses the grading window and roll-over rules. An ongoing window does not
become eligible before the date shown. An ended grading no longer qualifies
after it rolls over, but an unpromoted membership held at that grading can
still appear. A manually set promotion date takes precedence.

If your role includes **Promote Memberships**, the report can offer a promote
action for an eligible membership. Otherwise, ask someone with that access to
review and complete the promotion. After promoting, verify the member's new
level and promotion details on their membership.

## Example

To find all members ready for a particular next level, select the program and
next level, then review each eligible row against the program's requirements.
Do not treat a matching row as an automatic promotion; confirm the member's
record and follow your school's grading process.

For the complete level setup and promotion process, see
[Levels and Promotions](/programs/levels/).
