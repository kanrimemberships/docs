---
title: Only Memberships Report
description: Members who have an active membership but no active subscription.
---

Only Memberships identifies members with a current active membership who do
not have a currently active subscription. It is a comparison of present
membership and subscription states, not a list of memberships that were
unbilled at some point in the past.

The report uses the school's local current date to determine whether a
subscription is active. Archived, ended, and cancelled subscriptions do not
count as current. Memberships and subscriptions can be intentionally
independent, so a person appearing here does not by itself mean billing is
incorrect.

## Review a result

1. Open the member and check the active membership and its program.
2. Review the member's subscription history and payment arrangement.
3. If the member should have a subscription, follow your school's billing
   process. If they are intentionally paid another way or receive a
   complimentary membership, no change may be needed.

Use [Memberships](/reports/memberships/memberships/) for membership details and
[Subscriptions](/reports/billing/subscriptions/) for the subscription records
and their statuses.
