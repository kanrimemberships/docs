---
title: Only Subscriptions Report
description: Members who have an active subscription but no active membership.
---

Only Subscriptions identifies members with a currently active subscription but
no current active membership. It compares current records; it does not prove a
member is being charged today or that the subscription is unexpected.

The report uses the school's local current date. Archived, ended, and
cancelled subscriptions are not considered active. Review each result before
changing enrolment or billing: some subscription arrangements may be
intentional even when the member has no current membership.

## Review a result

1. Open the member's record and check for a current membership in any program.
2. Open the subscription and review its status, plan, dates, and recent
   payments.
3. If the subscription is intentional, follow your school's process for
   documenting that arrangement. If it is not, have an authorized staff
   member correct the membership or subscription.

Do not cancel a subscription solely because a row appears here. For full
subscription details, see [Subscriptions](/reports/billing/subscriptions/);
for the membership roster, see [Memberships](/reports/memberships/memberships/).
