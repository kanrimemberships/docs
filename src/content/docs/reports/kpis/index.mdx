This section will explain how to build key performance indicators (KPIs), set goals, and show KPI cards on your school's home page.
:::

A **KPI** is a measure you choose to keep an eye on, such as active members, new leads, or monthly revenue. You can define which records count, choose how Kanri calculates the number, and optionally track it against a goal over time.

## Planned guides

- [Create a KPI](/reports/kpis/create-kpi/): choose a measure, filter, and time period.
- [Set KPI Goals](/reports/kpis/set-goals/): set a fixed target, growth goal, deadline, or targets for individual periods.
- [Home Dashboard](/reports/dashboard/): choose and arrange KPI cards on the school's home page.
- [Configure Custom Report Charts and Summary Cards](/reports/custom-reports/configure-charts-and-stats/): use a saved KPI in a report.
