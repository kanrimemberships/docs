---
title: Disputed Payments Report
description: How to view and respond to member payment disputes (chargebacks).
---

Disputed Payments lists charge records in one of Kanri's dispute-related
statuses: **open**, **won**, **lost**, or **warning**. Its table shows the
invoice reference, member, amount, dispute reason, status, and response
deadline when available. A dispute generally means a charge has been
challenged or flagged, but this report does not replace the payment
provider's dispute process or its deadlines.

## Review a dispute

1. Review the response deadline and dispute reason shown in the row.
2. Open the invoice and member records to confirm the amount and transaction
   context.
3. Use your payment provider's account and your school's process to review
   the dispute, supply evidence, and track deadlines.
4. Return to Kanri to review the charge's updated status.

The report includes more than open disputes: a **won** or **lost** status is a
record of an outcome, while **warning** identifies a separate dispute-related
state. Do not infer the response deadline or evidence requirements from the
status label. Check the provider's instructions promptly.

For general charge records and CSV export, see
