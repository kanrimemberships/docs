---
title: Failing Subscriptions Report
description: How to find and act on subscriptions with failed payments.
---

Failing Subscriptions lists subscriptions whose latest invoiced scheduled
payment has a failed charge, while the subscription itself is still current.
The report does not mean every subscription with an old failed charge is
currently failing: the failed charge must be for that subscription's latest
invoiced payment.

## Review and follow up

1. Open the member and subscription to confirm the plan and current status.
2. Review the latest invoice and charge details to understand what failed.
3. Follow your school's process for contacting the member or updating their
   payment method.
4. Recheck the subscription and subsequent payment status before considering
   the issue resolved.

This report does not provide a bulk cancellation action or prescribe how long
to wait before cancelling. Do not cancel a subscription solely because it
appears here; use the billing record and your school's policy to decide the
next step. For all subscription records, see
[Subscriptions](/reports/billing/subscriptions/).
