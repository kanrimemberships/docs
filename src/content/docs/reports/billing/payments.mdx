---
title: Payments Report
description: How to review charge records by status and payment method.
---

Payments is a table of charge records. Use it to review charges and their
status, payment method, member, amount, and related billing details. It is a
charge report, so its rows are not necessarily equivalent to subscription
invoices or settled funds in an external payment provider.

## Filter and export

Use the **Status** and **Payment Method** filters to narrow the list. The
table is ordered newest first; review the **Date** column when you need to
locate a particular charge. Open a row's related invoice for more context and
download the filtered table as CSV. The built-in table does not provide a
date-range filter.

When calculating revenue, decide which charge statuses belong in your
calculation. A failed or disputed charge should not be treated as successfully
collected revenue just because it appears in the report. Refunds and other
adjustments may also affect the amount you ultimately reconcile.

## Example

To review a particular month's successful charges, build a
[Custom Report](/reports/custom-reports/) using **Charge** records, a
**Charged On** date range, and **Status is Success**, or use the Payments table
and review the dates shown.
Reconcile exported rows with your payment provider and accounting records.
For charge history over time, see
[Historical Payments](/reports/billing/historical-payments/); for disputes,
see [Disputed Payments](/reports/billing/disputed-payments/).
