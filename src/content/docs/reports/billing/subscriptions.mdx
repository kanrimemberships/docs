title: Subscriptions Report
description: A detailed breakdown of all subscriptions at your school — active, cancelled, and archived.
---

Subscriptions is a table of subscription records, including the member, plan,
price, start and end dates, next payment date, days to expiry, status, payment
method, and contract status when Documents is enabled. Use it to inspect a
subscription's dates and setup; do not treat the table as a statement of
settled payments.

## Search and filter

Search by member name or member record, or by billing plan. Other available
filters include days to expiry, payment method, subscription type, trial
status, payment amount, expiration date, cancellation date, conversion date,
and whether archived subscriptions are included. The type filter distinguishes
ongoing, paid-in-full, fixed-term, scholarship, session-block, and per-session
subscriptions.

Archived subscriptions are hidden unless you select **Show archived**. When
Memberships is enabled, the default list also focuses on subscriptions whose
members have an active membership; choose **Show inactive** to include
subscriptions for members without an active membership. Review the filter
state before treating the displayed rows as a complete billing audit.

The table can be downloaded as CSV. Use the export as a working list, then
reconcile charges and balances against the payment provider and your
accounting records.

## Example

To find current subscriptions ending soon, filter by the number of days to
expiry and review each end date and next payment date. To investigate a
subscription with a failed charge, open the member and subscription details,
then use [Failing Subscriptions](/reports/billing/failing-subscriptions/) to
find current failures.

For the historical count of active subscriptions over time, see
[Historical Subscriptions](/reports/billing/active-subscriptions/).
