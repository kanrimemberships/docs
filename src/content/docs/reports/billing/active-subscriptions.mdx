---
title: Historical Subscriptions Report
description: How to review active-subscription counts over time.
---

**Historical Subscriptions** charts the number of active subscriptions over
time, with an optional series for each
billing plan. It is a trend report, not a current list of subscription
records, and it does not calculate monthly recurring revenue.

## Read the chart

Use the time-period controls to review the available history. Group the chart
by billing plan when you want to see which plans account for the trend, or
view the total without a plan breakdown. You can also filter the source data
by billing plan.

Compare equivalent time spans and check whether a period is complete before
interpreting a rise or fall. The plotted values are counts of active
subscriptions on historical dates, not payment amounts or unique members.

For the current subscription records and their payment details, use
[Subscriptions](/reports/billing/subscriptions/). For a list of members with
subscriptions but no active membership, see
[Only Subscriptions](/reports/memberships/only-subscriptions/).
