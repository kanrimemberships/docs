---
title: Historical Payments Report
description: How to see your school's revenue trend over time.
---

Historical Payments charts charge amounts over time and can group the series
by payment method. Use it to spot changes in charge volume and timing. It is a
chart of charge records, not an accounting statement or confirmation of funds
settled by a payment provider.

## Compare periods

Choose a time period and, when useful, split the chart by payment method. For
example, group by payment method to compare card and bank charges, or leave
the chart as a single series to focus on the overall movement. For a fair
comparison, use equivalent date ranges and the same grouping. A partially
completed period may naturally show less activity than a full one.

The chart is best for understanding trends. For individual charges, statuses,
and CSV export, open [Payments](/reports/billing/payments/). Review
[Disputed Payments](/reports/billing/disputed-payments/) separately when
assessing charge disputes. If the chart changes sharply, use the same date
range in the Payments table or a [Custom Report](/reports/custom-reports/) to
investigate the underlying charge records. Confirm success, refunds, and
provider settlement independently before using the chart for financial
reconciliation.
