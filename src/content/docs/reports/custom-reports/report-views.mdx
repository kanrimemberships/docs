---
title: Manage Custom Report Views
description: Organize a report into tabs and decide whether each view is personal or shared.
---

A **view** is a tab inside a custom report. Use separate views to answer related questions
without putting every table and chart on one screen. For example, a **Member Overview** report
might have a **New Members** view and a **Membership Mix** view.

## Add a view

1. Open the report and use the add-view control beside its view tabs.
2. Enter a short **Name** that explains the view's purpose.
3. Choose **Personal (only me)** or **Shared (all viewers)**, then create the view.
4. Open the new tab and choose **Add Block** to add a table, chart, or stat.

If you started a report from scratch, create a view before adding its first block. Template
reports may already include a **Main** view.

## Personal and shared views

- A **Personal** view belongs to you and is visible only to you.
- A **Shared** view is available to people who can view the report, subject to their own
  report and record permissions.

The report's **Private** or **School Shared** setting is separate. A shared view inside a
private report does not make that report visible to other staff. See
[Share a Report](/reports/custom-reports/share-report/) before publishing a view for a team.

## Organize the view

Open a view tab's context menu to rename or delete it, or to change its icon. Drag the tabs to
change their order. People with report-management permission can also remove views they no
longer need.

Within a view, drag blocks to arrange charts and stats. The table is always shown after the
charts and stats, regardless of its stored position. Keep the table's columns and filters
focused on the detailed follow-up work that supports the summaries above it.

## Check what viewers can see

Staff need permission to view reports and to access the records and fields used by a view.
Kanri may therefore show different views or report data to staff with different roles. Ask
someone with the intended role to check the report before relying on it for a team process.
