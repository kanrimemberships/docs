---
title: Build Custom Report Filters
description: Use the filter builder to choose which school records appear in a custom report.
---

Filters decide which records appear in a table, chart, or stat. Each block has its own filter,
so a chart and table in the same view can analyze different subsets. Available records,
fields, and conditions depend on your school access and permissions.

## Add conditions

1. Choose the records for your report block.
2. Open **Filter** and add a condition, then choose a field.
3. Choose a comparison and enter or select its value. Available comparisons depend on the
   field type; enum fields use the options shown by the picker.
4. Use **All** when every condition must match, or **Any** when at least one condition is
   enough.
5. Check the results before saving. If you see no records, check each condition and whether
   **All** or **Any** matches your intention.

### Example: new leads to follow up

To find leads created this month, choose **Member** records and put these conditions in an
**All** group:

- **Member Type** is **Lead**.
- **Signed Up On** is in the current month.

This finds member profiles signed up during the current month. For membership records, choose
**Membership** and its **Enrolled On** field. That field shows when the membership record was
created, not when its status changed to active.

### Example: either of two conditions

Use **Any** when either condition should include a record. For example, a report for bookings
that are either confirmed or cancelled should use **Status is Confirmed** and
**Status is Cancelled** in an **Any** group. If you put them in **All**, one booking would
have to match both statuses at once, so it would not match either condition.

Connected records let you filter by related information, such as a booking's class or a
membership's program. Follow the relationship in the picker, then choose the field to compare.
A related record can be unavailable because it belongs to another school or was deleted; its
details do not become visible merely because another record refers to it.

## Let viewers adjust a filter

You can bind a supported filter value to a viewer input so staff can change that value
without editing the report:

1. Select the input link beside a filter value and choose **Create new input**, or bind to an
   input already in the block.
2. Open **Params** on the block to name the input, choose its type, and set a default value.
3. To make a condition optional, use **Only apply when…** and choose a toggle input.

An empty viewer input leaves its bound condition out of the query. A toggle that is off also
leaves its gated condition out. Check the defaults and preview the results so an unset input
does not make the report broader than you intend. Only compatible inputs are offered for a
filter value.

## Find attendance for a specific class date

Choose **Attendance** and combine these conditions in an **All** group:

- **Date** matches the required calendar date, for example October 27, 2026.
- **Class** or **Session** matches the class or recurring session you want.
- **Confirmed At** is not empty, if you want confirmed attendance only.

**Date** uses the timezone of the school where the person signed in. **Created At** is also
available when you need the original timestamp. Session schedules describe recurring weekly
slots; choosing a session alone does not restrict results to one calendar date.

Attendance can include your members' visits to other schools and visitors who signed in at
your school. Add **Signed-in School** for your school when counting people who attended at
this location. This differs from the built-in Attendance Report, which already limits results
to sign-ins at the selected school.

Schedules and session templates are also available for filtering and reporting, subject to
your existing permissions.

In workflows, **Date** can be used in filters and scheduled date-field triggers. A delay that
waits until a record's date field requires a stored date or timestamp, such as **Signed Up On**,
not the calculated attendance **Date**.
