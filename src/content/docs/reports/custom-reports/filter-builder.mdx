## Planned topics

- Choose a record type and find available fields.
- Add conditions and choose a comparison, such as is, contains, or is before.
- Combine conditions with **All** or **Any** rules, and group conditions when needed.
- Enter or select values for dates, amounts, names, statuses, and related records.
- Filter using fields from connected records, and understand when a field can have multiple matches.
- Check how a filter changes the results before saving.
- Troubleshoot empty results and conditions that don't match the records you expect.
