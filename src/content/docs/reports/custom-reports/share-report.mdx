---
title: Share a Custom Report
description: Make a report private or available to staff, while accounting for view and field permissions.
---

Report sharing controls who can open the report. It does not grant staff access to records or
fields that their role cannot otherwise view.

## Set report visibility

1. Open the report and choose its edit control. You need report-management permission.
2. Under **Visibility**, choose:
   - **Private** to keep the report available only to its creator.
   - **School Shared** to make it available to staff who have permission to view reports.
3. Save the changes.

A private report does not appear for other staff. A school-shared report can appear in their
Custom Reports list, but permission checks still apply to its views, fields, and actions.

## Report visibility is not view visibility

Each view also has its own **Personal (only me)** or **Shared (all viewers)** setting:

- The report must be **School Shared** before other staff can open it.
- A **Personal** view remains visible only to its owner, even inside a school-shared report.
- A **Shared** view can be seen by report viewers who also meet the view's permission
  requirements.

Use a personal view for your own layout or analysis. Use a shared view when the team needs the
same report tab. For details, see [Manage Report Views](/reports/custom-reports/report-views/).

## Review data access before sharing

The report builder offers only records and fields available under your permissions. Other
staff may have a narrower set of permissions, so they may not be able to open every view or
see every block. The report does not bypass their role permissions.

Before sharing:

1. Remove columns and calculations that staff do not need.
2. Check each block's filters and the date period they cover.
3. Ask a staff member with the intended role to open the report and confirm that the relevant
   views and data are available.
4. If they cannot access it, ask an administrator to review their role rather than changing
   permissions solely to make one report visible.

School sharing makes a report available within your school; it is not a public link.
