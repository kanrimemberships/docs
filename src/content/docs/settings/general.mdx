title: General Settings
description: Set your school's timezone, address, and default membership statuses.
---

General settings provide school details used throughout Kanri. You need **Manage Settings** to change them. Keep the timezone and address accurate because they affect how dates are shown and where payment hardware is registered.

## Set the timezone and default statuses

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select your school's **Timezone**.
3. If memberships are enabled, choose a **Default Active Membership Status** and **Default Inactive Membership Status**.
4. Select **Save**.

Choose the timezone where the school operates. This helps Kanri display class and payment dates in the right local time.

The active default is used for new active memberships. The inactive default is assigned when a membership ends. See [Membership Statuses](/settings/membership-statuses/) to manage the available labels.

## Update the school address

1. Open **Settings** and find the **Address** card.
2. Enter the school's street address, city, and postal or ZIP code.
3. Save the address.

The state or province and country come from the school's jurisdiction and cannot be changed in this card.

Keep the address current, especially if your school uses in-person card payments. For setup details, see [Set Up a Card Reader](/billing/terminal/set-up-reader/).
