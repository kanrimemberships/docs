---
title: Track Referral Sources
description: Record how a lead found your school and who referred them.
---

Kanri does not have a separate **Referral Types** settings list. Lead records store a free-text **Lead Source**, not a managed category. The current member form does not provide a field for staff to edit it. **Referred By** is a built-in evaluation question that can save the answer to the member record.

## Record a source or referral

To collect the name of the person who referred a prospective member, include the built-in **Referred By** question in your [evaluation form](/settings/evaluations/). That answer is saved to the member record; it is not entered in the general member-edit form.

For example:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Evaluation answer to Referred By | Jordan Lee |

The **Referred By** question asks who introduced the prospective member. Do not enter private details that are not useful for following up.

## Keep entries consistent

If Lead Source values are supplied through an integration or import, agree on consistent spellings with your team. The field is free text, not a settings list.

The Lead Funnel report tracks progress through lead milestones. It does not turn free-text referral entries into a managed referral-type list.
