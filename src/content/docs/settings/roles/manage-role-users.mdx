---
title: Manage Role Users
description: Review staff assigned to a role and change a staff member's role assignments.
---

Use the role's staff list to check who receives its permissions. The role page is for review; change role assignments from the staff member's record.

## Review staff assigned to a role

1. Open **Settings → Roles**.
2. Select the role.
3. Review the staff list and their contact details.
4. To change an assignment, open **Settings → Staff** and edit the staff member.

## Change a staff member's roles

1. Open **Settings → Staff** and select the staff member.
2. Choose **Edit**.
3. Select or clear the relevant roles.
4. Select **Save**.

After changing roles, ask the staff member to reload Kanri and confirm they can access the pages needed for their work. Avoid granting broad administrator access as a workaround for a single missing permission.

## If a staff member leaves

Remove their staff access from the staff record. Review their other roles and school assignments before considering the task complete.
