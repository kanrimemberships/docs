---
title: Roles
description: Use staff roles to control access to school features and records.
---

A role is a set of permissions assigned to staff. Permissions determine which areas a staff member can view or manage. A staff member can have more than one role, so review their combined access.

## Review roles

1. Open **Settings → Roles**.
2. Select a role to review its assigned staff.
3. Choose **Edit** to inspect or change the role's permissions.
4. Open **Settings → Staff** and edit a staff member to see or change their assigned roles.

## Create a role for a job

Create roles around the work people actually do. For example, a **Front Desk** role may need member and booking access, while an **Instructor** may need class and attendance tools. Give each role only the access required for those responsibilities.

Use [Create a Custom Role](/settings/roles/create-role/) to choose permissions. If a staff member needs broader access, update their assigned roles rather than sharing an owner's login.

## Assign or remove staff access

- [Invite a Staff Member](/settings/roles/invite-staff/) to add a new person.
- [Manage Role Users](/settings/roles/manage-role-users/) to review role assignments and change them from a staff record.

Clearing a role from a staff member's record removes the permissions granted by that role. If they have other roles, those permissions remain.
