---
title: Invite a Staff Member
description: Add an existing member or invite a new user to your school's staff.
---

Add staff from **Settings → Staff**. You can select an existing member or enter a new staff member's email address.
You need the **Manage Roles** permission to add staff.

## Add staff by email

1. Open **Settings → Staff** and select **Add Staff**.
2. Enter the person's email address.
3. Optionally enter their job title.
4. Select **Add Staff**.
5. A person with a verified Kanri account is added directly. Otherwise, Kanri sends an invitation email with account setup instructions.
6. After they are added, open their staff record and assign the appropriate roles.

## Add an existing member as staff

If the person already has a member profile, search for and select that member instead of entering an email. Kanri adds them directly if the profile is linked to an account; otherwise, it sends an invitation to the email on the member record.

If the member has no email address, add a valid email to their profile before inviting them.

## Give the person the right access

New staff should only receive the permissions needed for their role. Open **Settings → Staff**, select the staff member, choose **Edit**, and select one or more roles. See [Create a Custom Role](/settings/roles/create-role/).
