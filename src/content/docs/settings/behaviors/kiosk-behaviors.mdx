---
title: Kiosk Behaviors
description: Set the rules that control kiosk sign-in, mobile check-in, and attendance actions.
---

Kiosk behaviors control what happens when members or trial visitors check in. Open **Settings → Behaviors** and review the **Kiosk** group.

## Subscription and trial check-in

- **Block sign-ins with expired subscriptions** prevents sign-in when a member only has expired subscriptions.
- **Require a subscription to sign in** requires an active subscription.
- **Allow sign-ins before subscription starts** lets members with an active membership sign in before a subscription begins.
- **Allow trial members to sign in** lets members with trial bookings use kiosk check-in without a membership.

These rules affect kiosk access. Check membership and subscription status before changing a rule to resolve one member's issue.

## Attendance and mobile check-in

- **Require staff to confirm sign-ins** saves kiosk sign-ins as pending until staff confirms them.
- **Enable member mobile check-in** lets members check in from their own devices.
- **Require staff to confirm mobile check-ins** keeps those check-ins pending until staff confirms them.

See [Mobile Check-In](/kiosk/mobile-check-in/) for the member workflow and [Taking Attendance](/schedules/taking-attendance/) for staff follow-up.

## Kiosk actions and messages

The **Allow level promotions from attendance view** and **Allow photo updates from attendance view** settings let instructors promote member levels or update photos from the kiosk attendance view. **Allow promoting members who don't meet requirements** also permits promotion before a member meets the level's minimum classes or days requirements. Use these settings only if staff have a clear review process.

Use [Kiosk Messages](/settings/kiosk-messages/) to show a required message that must be acknowledged before sign-in or a banner notice on the confirmation screen. **Birthday celebration on sign-in** is another kiosk behavior. **Let visitors email themselves a waiver link** appears only when Documents is enabled, the intake page is enabled, and an intake waiver template is selected.
