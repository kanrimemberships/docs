title: General Behaviors
description: Control account linking, age-rule checks, and file uploads by member managers.
---

General behaviors affect how Kanri connects user accounts to member profiles and enforces age rules.

## Automatically link accounts to members

When **Automatically link users to members** is on, Kanri links a user to a member profile with a matching email address when the user signs in. Kanri links only when exactly one unlinked member at the school matches; if there are multiple matches, it skips the link. Linking lets that account access the member's self-service portal.

1. Open **Settings → Behaviors**.
2. Find **General**.
3. Turn on or off **Automatically link users to members**.
4. Confirm the saved setting.

Before enabling automatic linking, check that member email addresses are correct and not shared by unrelated people. If a link is incorrect, contact your school administrator for help correcting the account relationship.

## Choose where age restrictions apply

**Enforce age restrictions on** controls whether program and class minimum/maximum ages apply to bookings, kiosk sign-in, both, or neither. Review your school's booking and check-in process before changing this setting. The options are **Booking and kiosk**, **Booking only**, **Kiosk only**, and **Neither**.

## Allow member managers to upload files

**Allow managers to upload files** lets contacts who manage a member upload photos and documents for that member without staff doing the upload. Enable it only if that matches your school's privacy process. See [Member Gallery](/members/gallery/) and [Member Documents](/members/documents/).
