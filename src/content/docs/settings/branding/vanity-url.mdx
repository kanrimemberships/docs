---
title: School Subdomain
description: Set the unique Kanri subdomain used to reach your school's pages.
---

Your school subdomain is the school-specific part of its Kanri address. Set it in **Settings → Branding**, under **School Subdomain**. For example, a subdomain of `springfield-karate` appears as `springfield-karate` followed by Kanri's school domain.

You need owner access to change the school subdomain.

## Choose a subdomain

1. Enter a short, recognizable name using lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens.
2. Make sure it is 3 to 63 characters long.
3. Do not start or end it with a hyphen or use consecutive hyphens.
4. Save the Branding settings.

The subdomain must also be available and cannot use a reserved name. If Kanri reports that it is unavailable, try another variation.

## Update links that use the old address

Changing the subdomain changes your school's Kanri address. Update bookmarks, printed materials, and links you have shared outside Kanri. Before saving, check whether staff or members rely on the current address.

Use [Connected Domains](/settings/domains/) if you want to connect a domain your school owns instead of using the Kanri subdomain.
