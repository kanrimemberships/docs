title: Branding
description: Set the school identity, logo, color, email appearance, and sender details used in Kanri.
---

Branding helps members recognize messages and documents from your school. Open **Settings → Branding** to update your school's name, logo, color, email appearance, and school subdomain.

You need owner access to change Branding settings.

## Choose your school identity

- **School Name** is the school's name in Kanri.
- **Display Name** is an optional name used on lead and intake pages, sent documents, and emails. Leave it blank to use the School Name.
- **Email Sender ID** is the part before `@` in your outgoing email address.
- **School Subdomain** is your school's Kanri address.

See [Email Sender Address](/settings/branding/email-address/) and [School Subdomain](/settings/branding/vanity-url/) for setup details.

## Add a logo and brand color

Upload a logo or choose an image from the School Gallery. The logo appears on document PDFs and rich-text document pages. You can also choose whether to show it in outgoing emails.

Choose a brand color in the Branding card. Kanri uses it for buttons and other app elements. You can separately turn on **Apply brand color to emails**. See [Brand Colour and Logo](/settings/branding/brand-color-logo/).

## Adjust email appearance

The Email Branding section controls whether outgoing emails show your logo and brand color. The Email Footer section can include a Google review link when enabled. The referral link is available when its setting is enabled and your lead page is active.

## Add a school phone number

If your school uses Kanri messaging, see [SMS Phone Number](/settings/branding/phone-number/) to search for and select a dedicated number.
