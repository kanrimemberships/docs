---
title: Brand Colour and Logo
description: Add your school's logo and choose the color used in Kanri and outgoing email.
---

Set your school's logo and brand color in **Settings → Branding**.

You need owner access to change these settings.

## Add or change the logo

1. Select the logo control beside **School Identity**.
2. Upload one image file, or choose an image from the School Gallery.
3. Select **Save Logo**.
4. If you also want the logo in outgoing emails, turn on **Show logo in emails** in the Email Branding section and save it.

The school logo appears on document PDFs and rich-text document pages. The email option only controls email appearance.

## Choose a brand color

1. Find the **Branding** card.
2. Choose a color with the picker.
3. Select **Save**.
4. To apply it to emails as well, turn on **Apply brand color to emails** in the Email Branding section.

Kanri uses the color for buttons and other elements in the app. A color of `#000000` means no brand color. Choose a color that remains easy to read against the backgrounds where your school uses it.
