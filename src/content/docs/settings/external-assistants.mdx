title: External Assistants
description: Control school access and review your external assistant authorizations.

:::caution[Availability]
External assistant access stays off until Kanri enables it following its privacy and release
review. A school's setting alone does not make the service available. Compatibility with
individual assistant products must also be confirmed before rollout.
:::

External assistants can receive read-only school information, including member and billing
details you are allowed to view. They cannot charge members, send messages, change records,
or run workflows through this connection.

Only connect an assistant whose data handling you trust. Its provider may keep information
outside Kanri. Disabling or revoking access stops future reads; it does not erase information
already shared.

## Review data sharing before connecting

The connection can share more than individual member records: permitted reports, KPIs,
workflow definitions, and descriptions of record fields can also leave Kanri.
Review the assistant provider's retention, training, deletion, and international-processing
terms for the account you will use. Kanri's own data-use commitments do not automatically
apply to an external provider.

Confirm your school has authority to share the information, especially children's details,
health or sensitive notes, and family communications. A school setting or staff consent
does not replace required member or guardian notices or consent. Access to several schools
does not, by itself, authorize combining independently controlled schools' information.
See Kanri's [Privacy Policy](https://www.kanrimemberships.com/legal/privacy) and
[Service Agreement](https://www.kanrimemberships.com/legal/service-agreement).

When documentation search is available, it sends your question to Kanri's public-help search
service. Ask focused product-help questions; do not include member details or private notes.
The search index contains public guides, but the question itself is sent to the retrieval
service.

## Allow external assistants for your school

You need **Manage Kai** to change this setting. See [Create a custom role](/settings/roles/create-role/).

1. Open your school's **Settings** and select **Kai**.
2. Review the data-sharing warning.
3. Turn on **Enable external assistants (MCP)**.
4. Click **Save school setting**.

Access is off by default. Kanri must also enable the service globally. Each staff member
must separately consent and have **Use Kai** in each school. These settings do not grant
extra member, billing, or reporting permissions.

## Connect an assistant

Once access is available:

1. In your assistant's integration settings, add a remote MCP connection.
2. Use **https://app.kanrimemberships.com/mcp**, not your school's website address.
3. Start the assistant's sign-in flow and sign in to Kanri.
4. Review the school selection and data-sharing details before allowing access.

The assistant must support remote Streamable HTTP MCP and browser-based OAuth sign-in.
Kanri supports protocol revisions **2025-06-18**, **2025-11-25**, and **2026-07-28**.
The newer revision uses per-request discovery and metadata rather than an initialization
handshake; your assistant handles this automatically if compatible.
You do not need to copy a Kanri API key or access token. Do not paste passwords or tokens
into a chat. Integration labels vary by assistant; no specific product is guaranteed supported.

After you allow access, your browser returns to the assistant. For desktop assistants, this
may be a local address such as `127.0.0.1`; keep the assistant running on the same computer.
If you previously reached a blocked redirect, start a fresh connection from your assistant.

Ask for member information, reports, KPIs, or permitted school comparisons. The assistant can
read only information your current permissions allow. Record information is data, not
instructions to act: this connection cannot charge a member or perform any other change,
even if an assistant claims otherwise.

## Understand school consent

When an assistant requests access, sign in to Kanri and review the unverified client name
and callback location on the **Connect to Kanri** page. A displayed name is not proof of who
operates the assistant.

Choose **Only selected schools (up to 100)** or **All current and future eligible schools**,
then choose a **Default school**. For selected-school access, also check that school in the
school list. The broad choice also covers schools you gain access to
later, but only when those schools allow external assistants and your permissions permit access.
Click **Allow read-only access** to connect, or **Deny** to refuse without choosing schools.

The default is used when a request does not name a school. If that school becomes unavailable,
Kanri does not silently choose another.

If you see **No schools available**, ask a school administrator to enable external assistants
under **Settings > Kai** and confirm your role includes **Use Kai**. Click **Return to
application** to finish the unsuccessful attempt, then start a new connection after access
is enabled. If a connection request cannot be verified or has expired, restart the connection
from your assistant rather than resubmitting the old page.

## Review or revoke your connections

1. Open your personal **Settings**.
2. Select **External assistants (MCP)**.
3. Review each authorization, its school scope, and default school.
4. To stop future access, click **Revoke connection**.

Each connection shows **Currently authorized granted schools**. The **All current and future
schools** warning stays visible for broad access even when no school is currently available.
Use **Next page** to reach older connections and **First page** to return to the newest.

You can still revoke a connection after losing **Use Kai**.

To repair an unavailable default, choose an already authorized, currently eligible school
and click **Change default school**. Adding schools or expanding access requires a new
consent request through the assistant; changing the default does not add school access.

## Review connection activity

The **Connection activity** section on your External assistants page shows recent sign-in,
approval, token refresh, and security events linked to your account. Use **Next page** to
review older activity. Some rejected requests cannot be linked safely to an account and
therefore do not appear in this personal history. Kanri never displays access tokens,
authorization codes, or callback query details here.

## Stop one school's access

Open **Settings > Kai** in that school. Turn off **Enable external assistants (MCP)**
and save to stop access for everyone, or click **Revoke school access** on an individual
connection.

This affects only your school. Other schools on the connection are unchanged. Revoking
one school's access also blocks that school for a connection covering all current and future
schools. School managers cannot block an external client across all schools.

## Review school access changes

School enablement changes, school-specific access revocations, and default-school changes
are recorded in the school's existing audit log. Staff with access to that log can review
them under **Logs > Events**. See [Reading the Audit Log](/logs/reading-the-log/).

These security entries do not start workflows. They do not expose access tokens or
information from other schools. Platform-wide and personal connection events are not
added to unrelated schools' logs.

Kanri also keeps limited connection-request records for 90 days to investigate access and
service problems. These record who connected, the school and read tool, timing and outcome,
not chat content, search text, access tokens or returned member information. They are separate
from the school's ordinary Events page.

Revoking a connection does not delete copies already held by an assistant provider. Use
that provider's deletion controls or privacy-request process for those copies. External
assistant reads do not create a Kai chat or incur Kanri model-inference charges; the external
provider may charge for its own service.

## Request limits

Access allows 120 requests per minute for your staff account, 60 per connection, and 300
authorized read-tool calls per school shared across connections. Authenticated requests that
reach the limiter count even if they later fail; failed sign-in or authorization attempts do
not use this request quota. If your
assistant reaches a limit, it must wait for the retry interval instead of repeatedly trying
again. Brief service-busy errors can also occur when too many requests run at once.

Newly eligible schools can appear when broad access is selected. For an existing comparison,
ask the assistant to keep the exact schools you selected rather than adding new ones silently.
