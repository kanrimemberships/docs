---
title: Set Up Billing
description: How to create a billing plan, add a payment method to a member, and create their first subscription in Kanri.
---

Billing in Kanri works in three layers:

1. **Billing Plan**: the named product you're selling (e.g. "Monthly Karate").
2. **Price**: the specific amount and payment schedule on that plan (e.g. C$120/month).
3. **Subscription**: a member's agreement to pay a specific price, processed automatically.

This guide walks through creating a billing plan and attaching a subscription to a member for the first time.

## Before you start

Connect Stripe before collecting card or bank payments through Kanri. You can still record subscriptions for cash, e-transfer, or other payments made outside Kanri without connecting Stripe.

## Step 1: Create a billing plan

When you create a billing plan, you give it a name and set up its default price at the same time.

1. Go to **Billing → Billing Plans**.
2. Click **New Billing Plan**.
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Name**: what you call this plan (e.g. "Monthly Karate" or "Annual Membership").
   - **Plan Type**: choose how payments work.
     - **Ongoing**: charges repeat until the subscription is cancelled.
     - **Fixed Term**: charges repeat for a set number of payments, then stop.
     - **Paid in Full**: a single one-time payment.
   - **Payment Amount**: the charge amount per payment.
   - **Payment Interval Count** and **Payment Interval**: together these define how often to charge. For example, enter `1` and `Month` for monthly billing, or `2` and `Week` for every two weeks.
   - **Number of Payments**: (Fixed Term only) how many times to charge before the subscription ends.
4. Click **Create Plan**.

Kanri creates the plan with one default price. If you charge different amounts for different members — for example, adult vs. junior rates — you can add more prices from the plan's page after creating it. You'll need to enable this in [Subscription Behaviors](/settings/behaviors/subscription-behaviors/) first.

:::tip
On Professional and Enterprise plans, you'll also see a **Contract Template** field. This lets you attach a document that members must sign when a subscription is created. You can set this up later under Documents.
:::

## Step 2: Add a payment method to the member

For members paying by card or bank account, you need a payment method on file before creating the subscription.

1. Open the member's profile and go to **Billing → Payment Methods**.
2. Click **Add Payment Method**.
3. Give it a **Label** and choose who will pay in the **Bill To** field.
4. Choose **Email Link** to send a secure link, or **Collect via Terminal** if your school has a Stripe Terminal reader.
5. The payer enters their card or bank details through the secure setup.

Once the member submits their details, the payment method appears on their profile as active.

:::note
The payment link is sent by email. Make sure the payer can receive it before choosing this option.

If a member pays by cash, e-transfer, or another method outside Kanri's payment processing, you can skip this step and select the appropriate payment type when creating the subscription.
:::

## Step 3: Create a subscription

1. Go to **Billing → Subscriptions** and click **New Subscription**.
2. Select the **Billing Plan** you created.
3. If your school has multiple prices on a plan, select the **Price** (e.g. "Adult").
4. Select the **Payment Method** if the member has one on file.
5. Set the **Start Date**: when the subscription begins.
6. Set the **First Payment Date**: when the first charge is made. This defaults to the start date, so you only need to change it if you want to delay the first payment.
7. Click **Create Subscription**.

If the subscription has a payment method for automatic collection, Kanri attempts to charge on the scheduled payment dates. Otherwise, the subscription remains available for record-keeping and offline payment tracking.

## Confirming it worked

After saving, the subscription appears under **Billing → Subscriptions**. Its status depends on the start date and other subscription details. If Kanri attempts a payment, you can review it in the member's payment history.

If a payment fails, the subscription will appear in the [Failing Subscriptions report](/reports/billing/failing-subscriptions/).
