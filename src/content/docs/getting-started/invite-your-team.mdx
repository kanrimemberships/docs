title: Invite Your Team
description: How to create staff roles and invite team members to your school in Kanri.
---

Kanri uses roles to control staff access. Adding someone as staff and assigning their permissions are separate steps. Create or review the role, add the person, then assign the role on their staff record.

## Before you start

You need permission to manage roles. Only owner-tier staff can assign owner-level access or change school assignments. Use a separate account for each team member rather than sharing an owner's login.

## Step 1: Create a role (if needed)

Your school may already have built-in administrative roles. Review them before creating another one. For routine work, create a narrower role, such as *Instructor* or *Front Desk*, instead of giving everyone unrestricted access.

1. Go to **Settings → Roles**.
2. Click **Add Role**.
3. Give the role a name (e.g. "Instructor", "Front Desk", "Admin").
4. Toggle on the permissions this role should have. Permissions are grouped by area (Members, Billing, Schedules, etc.).
5. Click **Create**.

Choose at least one permission. You cannot grant permissions you do not have yourself. See [Permissions](/reference/permissions/) for what each permission controls.

:::tip
Start with a conservative set of permissions and add more if staff tell you they need access to something. It's easier to grant access than to explain why something was visible that shouldn't have been.
:::

## Step 2: Add a staff member

1. Go to **Settings → Staff**.
2. Click **Add Staff**.
3. Search for an existing member, or enter the person's email address. Choose the existing member when you want to use that person's linked account.
4. Optionally enter a **Job Title**.
5. Click **Add Staff**.

What happens next depends on the person's situation:

- **Selected member with a linked account:** Kanri adds that account as staff directly.
- **Email address already verified on a Kanri account:** Kanri adds the existing account directly.
- **Selected member without a linked account, or an email without a verified account:** Kanri sends an invitation. The selected member must have an email address. Ask the person to accept the invitation; it expires after seven days.

If an invitation expires, repeat **Add Staff** with the correct email to send a fresh one. Adding staff does not assign a role, so continue with the next step once the staff record is available.

## Step 3: Assign the role

1. Open **Settings → Staff** and select the staff member.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Under **Roles**, select the role or roles the person needs.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Ask the person to sign in and check that they can access the intended school and tools.

## What the staff member can see

Access depends on the person's combined roles and the features enabled for your school. A role does not enable a feature your school does not have. If something is missing, check both the role's permissions and the school's feature availability.

## Managing your team

Open a role under **Settings → Roles** to see its users. To change one person's assignments, open their staff record, select **Edit**, change **Roles**, and save.

A person can have several roles. Removing one role does not remove access granted by another. Kanri also protects the last owner from losing owner access.
