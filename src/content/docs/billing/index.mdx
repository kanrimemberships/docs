Kanri keeps the rules for what you charge separate from the records of what a member owes and pays. Understanding those pieces makes it easier to set up billing and investigate a payment later.

## How the pieces fit together

1. A **billing plan** names an offering, such as *Adult Karate* or *Family Membership*.
2. A **price** defines how that offering is charged: its amount, schedule, and price type. A plan must have a price.
3. A **subscription** assigns a member to a plan and price. It also records when billing starts and, when applicable, which payment method to use.
4. An **invoice** records an amount due. Invoices can be one-time bills, or can be created as part of subscription billing.
5. A **charge** records a payment attempt or a payment recorded by staff. One invoice can have more than one charge, for example after an unsuccessful attempt followed by a successful one.
6. A **payout** is a transfer from Stripe to the school's bank account. A successful charge and a bank payout are separate steps.

For example, a member's C$120 monthly subscription is a subscription to a plan and price. When a scheduled payment is due, Kanri records it as an upcoming subscription payment. Kanri creates an invoice for automatic collection only when the subscription has a saved payment method; staff can use **Invoice Now** on an upcoming payment to create an invoice for another collection method. An auto-sent contract template also holds invoice creation until the contract is signed. Stripe processes supported online payments, while Kanri keeps the invoice and charge history.

## Choose the right billing record

| Use this | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscription** | A member's ongoing or scheduled billing arrangement, such as monthly tuition. |
| **Invoice** | A specific amount to collect, such as a uniform purchase, registration fee, or amount owed outside a subscription. |
| **Charge** | The record of a payment attempt or recorded payment against an invoice. |

Use an invoice for a specific amount, whether it is a one-time bill or an invoice created by subscription billing. Use a subscription when you need Kanri to schedule billing according to a price. See [Invoices](/billing/invoices/) and [Subscriptions](/members/subscriptions/) for the full workflows.

## Billing plans and prices

A plan can have one price or several, depending on **Billing plan mode** in **Settings → Behaviors**:

- **Single**: each plan has one price. Edit the plan to change its price details. If the pricing terms change, existing subscriptions keep the price assigned to them; review the member subscriptions before changing a plan's price.
- **Multiple**: a plan can have several prices, for example adult and junior rates or monthly and annual options. The plan has a default price for new subscriptions, and staff can choose a price when creating a subscription.

Price types include **Ongoing**, **Paid in Full**, **Fixed Term**, **Scholarship**, **Session Block**, and **Per Session**. They are not interchangeable: a session block tracks prepaid visits, while per-session pricing records usage for billing. Review [Billing Plans](/billing/billing-plans/) before creating an offering.

## Collecting and recording payments

Kanri can use a saved payment method, send an online payment link, or start a payment on a connected card reader. Staff can also record cash or mark an invoice as paid when money was collected outside Kanri. **Mark as paid records a payment; it does not charge a card or move funds.**

Before trying a second payment method, check the invoice's charge history. A saved-method or reader charge may still be processing. Starting another attempt too soon can lead to duplicate collection.

See [Collect a Payment](/billing/invoices/collect-payment/) for the options and how to handle partial or pending payments.

## Stripe, payouts, and payment problems

Kanri stores billing plans, subscriptions, invoices, payment history, and the settings it needs to start supported payment flows. Stripe processes online payments and provides the connected-account controls; use the information in **Settings → Payouts** for payout status.

- Set up the connected Stripe account before collecting card or bank payments through Kanri.
- Review payout and bank-account details in **Settings → Payouts** or **Settings → Account**.
- For a payment that did not succeed, check its charge or invoice status before retrying. Kanri's subscription retry schedule is configured under **Settings → Behaviors → Billing Notifications**; it is not a promise that Stripe will independently retry the payment.
- Handle a card dispute in Stripe. Kanri's [Disputed Payments report](/reports/billing/disputed-payments/) can help locate the affected payment.

For steps, see [Payouts](/billing/payouts/), [Failed Payments](/billing/troubleshooting/failed-payments/), and [Disputed Payments](/billing/troubleshooting/disputed-payments/).
