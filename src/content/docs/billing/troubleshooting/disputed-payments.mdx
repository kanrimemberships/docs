---
title: Disputed Payments
description: Find a disputed payment in Kanri and respond to the dispute through Stripe.
---

A dispute is not the same as a failed payment or a refund request. Kanri helps you locate the affected invoice and charge; it does not provide the dispute-response form.

## Find the affected payment

1. Open the [Disputed Payments report](/reports/billing/disputed-payments/).
2. Find the member, invoice, and charge connected to the dispute.
3. Open the invoice or member's [charge history](/members/billing/view-charges/) to confirm the payment details.
4. Open the connected Stripe account and locate the matching dispute.

Kanri helps identify the affected billing records. Use the connected-account controls to read the instructions shown for that dispute and follow the process there. This guide cannot confirm a deadline or response requirement for an individual account.

## Prepare a response

Use the evidence requested for the dispute type. Depending on the case, relevant records may include:

- the invoice and itemized description of what was charged
- the subscription or agreement showing the member's billing arrangement
- receipts and the payment history
- relevant communications with the member
- evidence that a product or service was provided, where applicable

Submit any requested response through Stripe using the instructions shown there. Keep copies of what you submit and record any follow-up with the member.

## After you respond

Check the dispute status in Stripe and the corresponding charge in Kanri. Update the member's billing records only when the outcome or a separate refund requires it. Do not issue a second refund just because a dispute is open.

For a payment that failed rather than being disputed, see [Failed Payments](/billing/troubleshooting/failed-payments/). For payout questions, see [Payouts](/billing/payouts/).
