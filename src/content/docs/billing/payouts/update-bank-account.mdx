---
title: Update Your Bank Account
description: Change or review payout bank details for your school's connected Stripe account.
---

Payout bank details are managed in the school's connected Stripe account, not in a Kanri billing plan or member profile. Only an authorized school owner should make changes.

## Open Stripe account management

1. Go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Open the Stripe account-management panel.
3. Find the payout bank-account or external-account section in Stripe.
4. Follow Stripe's steps to add or update the account and complete any requested verification.
5. Confirm the new details and any default-account selection in Stripe before leaving the page.

Kanri embeds Stripe's account-management interface; the available bank-account controls and any instructions are provided there. If the relevant controls are unavailable or unclear, use the support options in that connected-account interface rather than entering bank information elsewhere.

## After you change the account

Check **Settings → Payouts** to review the payout information supplied by the connected account.

For a payout-specific question, review the details in Stripe and use the support options shown there. Contact Kanri support if the account-management panel is unavailable.

For payout status and timing, see [Payouts](/billing/payouts/).
