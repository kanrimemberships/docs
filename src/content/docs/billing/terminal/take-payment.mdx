---
title: Take an In-Person Payment
description: Collect an invoice payment on a connected card reader and confirm its result.
---

Use **Pay on reader** when the member is present and ready to pay by card. The reader flow can also offer Interac debit for a Canadian school. The charge is linked to the invoice, so check the invoice balance before you begin.

## Start the payment

1. Open the member's invoice. If it is a draft, open it before charging.
2. Check the balance due and the Charges section. If a charge is still processing, wait for its result rather than starting another one.
3. Select **Charge**, then choose **Pay on reader**.
4. Select the reader. If more than one is registered, confirm the label matches the device in front of you.
5. Confirm the amount. For a partial payment, enter only the amount being collected.
6. If needed, select **Send receipt email** and confirm the email address.
7. Select **Charge on Reader** and ask the member to present their card or, if offered, Interac debit card to the reader.

## Confirm the result

Keep the invoice page open while the reader payment is processing. Wait until Kanri reports the result, then review the charge in the invoice's **Charges** section:

- A successful charge reduces the invoice balance by the amount received.
- A partial payment leaves the remaining balance due.
- A failed charge does not count as money received.
- A processing charge is not a final result. Do not repeat the payment until you have checked whether it succeeded or failed.

If you need to leave the page, use the available background-wait option and return to check the status before starting another attempt.

## If the reader option is unavailable

The reader option requires terminal setup and at least one registered reader. Check **Settings → Terminal** and follow [Set Up a Card Reader](/billing/terminal/set-up-reader/). If the device is listed but unavailable, review its status and contact Kanri support if you cannot resolve the connection.

For cash, saved payment methods, payment links, or recording a payment collected elsewhere, see [Collect a Payment](/billing/invoices/collect-payment/).
