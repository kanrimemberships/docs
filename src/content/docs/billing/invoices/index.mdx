An invoice records a specific bill and its amount due. It can include products, services, or custom line items, or be created for a scheduled subscription payment. A [subscription](/members/subscriptions/) defines the billing arrangement and schedule.

Each payment attempt is a **charge**. One invoice can have multiple charges, such as a failed attempt followed by a successful payment. You can review these from the invoice or a member's [Charges](/members/billing/view-charges/) page.

An invoice, its charges, and its fulfillment answer different questions:

| Record | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice | What was sold or billed, who owes it, and the total due |
| Charge | What happened when someone tried to pay, including amount, method, and result |
| Fulfillment | Whether a physical product is reserved, waiting for stock, or handed over |

For example, a uniform invoice can be paid while the uniform is still waiting for delivery. Check the charge to confirm payment, then check [fulfillment](/shop/fulfil-sales/) to see whether the member has collected the item.

## Create and manage invoices

- [Create an Invoice](/billing/invoices/create-invoice/): bill a member or record a walk-in shop sale.
- [Edit an Invoice](/billing/invoices/edit-invoice/): update an eligible draft or open invoice.
- [Collect a Payment](/billing/invoices/collect-payment/): take or record payment, including partial payments.
- [Refund or Void a Payment](/billing/invoices/refunds-and-voids/): refund a completed charge or void an unpaid invoice.
- [Receipts and Payment History](/billing/invoices/receipts-and-history/): download or send invoice and payment receipts.

For selling physical products, continue to [Shop and Inventory](/shop/). Learn how to [fulfill a product sale](/shop/fulfil-sales/) or [process a return](/shop/returns/).

## Find the right invoice list

The **Invoices** page has **My Invoices**, **Shop**, **Subscriptions**, and **All** views. The default **Shop** view shows shop invoices, including product sales. Use **Subscriptions** for subscription billing records and **All** when you need to search across sources. **My Invoices** is the member-facing view for the signed-in member's invoices (or invoices they can manage), not a staff member's personal billing list.

An invoice marked **Draft** has not been opened for payment yet. Opening it makes it available for payment but does not collect money; depending on the school's invoice-email setting, opening it can also send the invoice link. Check the invoice's balance and charge history before deciding whether another payment attempt is needed.
