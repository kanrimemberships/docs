---
title: Manage Gradings
description: Schedule grading dates, review eligible members, and promote members in a group.
---

Gradings let you schedule an event for one or more programs, review members due to advance, and promote eligible members together.

Grading management is available to staff with program-view access. Members can see scheduled gradings for their active programs in the calendar, but cannot open the grading roster or management pages.

## Create a grading

1. Open **Programs → Gradings** and select **New Grading**.
2. Enter a name and date and time. The time shown and submitted is interpreted directly in the school's time zone, regardless of your device's time zone.
3. Select one or more programs.
4. Optionally enable **Close the selected programs for this grading**.
5. Select **Create Grading**.

When you close the selected programs, Kanri creates a closed day for the grading's date. It blocks attendance, student bookings, and trial bookings for those programs. Editing the grading updates the linked closed day.

## Review the roster

Open a grading to see summary counts and separate, paginated lists:

- **Confirmed eligible** includes members who meet both the minimum-days and attendance requirements, plus anyone manually included. Attendance is counted through the grading's date in the school's time zone.
- **May be eligible** includes members who meet one requirement but not both. This includes members who have attended enough classes but still need more time at their current level.
- **Not met** includes members who meet neither requirement.

Only active memberships whose current level is marked for grading and has a next level appear in these lists. Members already promoted in this grading are removed from the roster.

Use **Include manually** from the **May be eligible** or **Not met** list to bypass the remaining thresholds. Manual inclusion does not bypass the need for an active membership, a current level marked for grading, and a next level. You can remove manual inclusion from the Confirmed eligible list.

Use **Exclude from this grading** to leave a member off one grading's promotion list without changing their eligibility elsewhere. Excluded members remain visible in that grading, and **Restore to grading** reverses the exclusion. Excluded members cannot be selected for promotion.

After a grading starts, eligible members who were not promoted can appear in the next eligible future grading. They remain visible on the earlier grading's roster. Once a member is promoted, Kanri keeps their earlier grading eligibility and level details in that historical roster.

## Promote members

Members on the current **Confirmed eligible** page are selected by default. Uncheck anyone absent, then select **Promote N selected one level** to advance only the remaining selections. Selections apply only to the current page. Kanri rechecks eligibility when processing promotions and advances each selected member by one level. A member cannot be promoted twice in the same grading.

Members and kiosk screens identify next promotion dates tied to gradings with **(grading)**. These dates are calculated from each level's setting and upcoming gradings; they are not saved as membership dates. A member-specific promotion date takes precedence.
