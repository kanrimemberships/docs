---
title: Promotions Report
description: How to use the Promotions report to find members who are ready to be promoted to the next level.
---

The Promotions report lists active memberships ready to advance and shows their current and next levels, attendance, and next promotion date. Dates based on a grading are labeled **(grading)**.

Leave **Eligible by** empty to see members who meet their date and class requirements today. Choose a date and click **Search** to check date eligibility by that cutoff; this filter does not require the class threshold to be met, so review the attendance count before promoting.

Use the other report filters to narrow the list by program, current level, or next level. To manage grading rosters or promote a group for a scheduled event, see [Manage Gradings](/programs/levels/gradings/).
