---
title: Promotions Report
description: How to use the Promotions report to find members who are ready to be promoted to the next level.
---

The Promotions report lists members with promotion history and shows their current and next levels, attendance, and next promotion date. Dates based on an upcoming grading are labeled **(grading)**.

Use the report filters to narrow the list by program or level. A next-promotion date does not by itself mean a member meets the class threshold; review the attendance count before promoting them. To manage grading rosters or promote a group for a scheduled event, see [Manage Gradings](/programs/levels/gradings/).
